*All memos of the Office of the Counterweight are read aloud at the Saltmouth exchange each Forge-day, per regulation, then nailed inside the ledger cupboard, per regulation, then read a second time by whoever opens the cupboard for chalk, which is not regulation but is how anything gets read. Copied here in the order found.*

## Memo 1/Season: On Calibration

The brass scale is to be calibrated each dawn against the trial weights, which are themselves calibrated against the master weight, which is calibrated against the scale. An apprentice has asked whether this is a circle. It is not a circle. It is a *relationship*, and it has outlasted four guildmasters. Any staff member introducing a fifth object into this relationship will be weighed and entered as spoilage.

## Memo 2/Season: On the Weighing of Souls

It has come to the Office's attention that a courier told a new debtor that soul-weighing at contract is "a bit we do." It is not a bit. It is a standard practice of long standing, performed at the moment of signature, and the Guild insurance schedule contains a clause — schedule nine, paragraph four — that prices a man's labour differently depending on the reading. Ask why Ledger Office underwrites souls and you will receive a Form 22-B. Fill it in. That is what it is for. That is what *everything* is for, eventually.

Newly contracted persons are reminded: stand still, breathe out, and do not attempt to think lighter thoughts. The beam reads intention, and intention is denser than it looks.

## Memo 4/Season: On Inventory Columns

Effective immediately, stocktake will be conducted in the following order: spoilage, surplus, stock, and only then the shelves. The Ledger Office has queried the order. The Ledger Office may continue to query it. Answers will be provided when the Ledger Office can explain why a column balanced before the goods arrived for three consecutive weeks in the month of Fog. Awaiting response. Not holding breath. Breath is a numbered asset.

## Memo 7/Season: RE: THE CRATE

Weighed it.

Read the beam yourself if you want. It will still be saying it tomorrow. It says everything tomorrow first.

The Office will take no queries. The Office is *counting backwards*, and asks not to be counted until it is finished.

— S.C., Auditor-Titular
