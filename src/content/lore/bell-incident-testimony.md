*Lamp-book office, temple steps, Lamp Rest. Filed under D, at last, by order of the Disputation (see Minutes, Forty-First: "attendance ... one ear"). The parties discussed it once, for the record, on the condition that it be put away after. It has been. You are holding the away where it was put.*

## Deposition of Brother Decibel, sound-keeper

"I was reaming the west horn's throat. Inversion night, dead calm, and the throat had gone fouled with dust, same as every calm night since money. The horn is an *ear* — enter it that way — so I go in ear-first. Habit. The clerk has written *regrettable habit* and I have struck *regrettable* and the clerk has allowed it.

"What I heard first was not from the horn's mouth. It was from its *skin* — the brass itself, humming, the way a kettle hums before it admits to boiling. Then the mouth: *sound came out.* Thirty-one years I have oiled that gimbal so the horn could take the world in. The world, that night, came back out.

"I remember it kindly, is the strange part. It was not loud in the way of thunder. It was loud in the way of *somebody standing up.* My left ear stopped committee work that evening and never rejoined. The clerk asks whether I hold the horn responsible. I do not. You do not blame a door for what knocks."

## Statement of Static-Warden Pem

"Entered as weather at the time, against my initials, which I lent under protest of the mildest kind: none.

"The litanies for that night: band three, clear; band seven, clear; band zero — the band that is not a band — four seconds of carrier, shaped like the Sleeve's Own but *answered*, station. One long call is the Sleeper-call; the litanies hold it reserved because it is not ours. What I recorded on band zero was one long call, and then, nine seconds later, *the same call, from down the crater wall.* An echo of nine seconds requires a reflector some kilometres distant, flat as a tabletop, facing us exactly. The crater floor was surveyed in my grandmother's youth. It has no such face.

"It has no such face *standing up.*"

## The horn's own testimony, transcribed

The west horn's emission, per both depositions and one lamp-lighter who flatly slept through it, lasted eleven seconds and contained, in order:

One. Birdsong. The clerk notes in the margin: *Kessa-9 has no birds. Entered without remedy.*

Two. A woman laughing, once, at a joke no one present arrived in time to hear. Decibel volunteers, and the record accepts, that it was a *nice* laugh, and that this was the worst part.

Three. One long tone — the Sleeve's Own — held, and then *bent upward a half-step*, the way you raise your voice at the end of a question.

## Ruling

Filed as weather, on the grounds that weather is the filing drawer with the fewest consequences. The horn ring re-blessed; the west horn permitted its pride; Decibel instructed to wear the patch and the men instructed not to ask; both instructions have held. The Disputation's closing note stands as the finding of this office: *we heard.*

Marginal, later hand, believed Pem's: *It asked the question before the courier was born to carry the answer. We are all of us early for something. Amen, and copy to the kettle.*
