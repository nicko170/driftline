## Hover springs
Damping is not a preference. Under-damped: you porpoise over dunes until you learn the desert through your spine. Over-damped: every rock gets a vote. Correct: one bob, one settle, like a man sitting down who's done this before. If your bike *settles twice*, bring it in. If it settles **three times**, sell it to the Reclaimers and don't tell them why.

## Gyro rings
Gyro rings develop Opinions. This is normal. This is documented. A ring's Opinion is expressed as a faint lean you'll swear is the road. Rebalance quarterly. If the Opinion survives rebalancing, it has been promoted to Character, and Character is not a fault, it is a *relationship*. See also: service record of the bike currently known as the Wren — owner one's entries, season 31. I have consulted that page so often it's gone soft.

## Why the Wren pulls left
Because she pulls left. Because three owners have written "pulls left" in her log and each then wrote, within a season, "have stopped noticing" (owner two wrote "have started aiming right to compensate, wife says I now walk funny"). Because machines that survive the Cinderflats get to keep one eccentricity the way couriers keep one scar they lie about. Alignment is perfect. The pull is *editorial*. Ride around it. Talk to her if it helps; it helps.

## Boost cells
Boost is a loan, not a wage. Spend it climbing out of trouble, not diving in. A rider who boosts into a canyon wall has invented a new kind of tax and I charge it as labour.

## Horizon cell
Do not open it. Do not tap it. It hums; hums back only while stationary; do not ask me how I know that. Verdantine built three things that outlived the money, and the cell is the one that scares me least. Top billing.

## General
A bike is a coat you wear at a hundred and ten kilometres an hour. Maintain it like one. Wash the salt off before the salt becomes load-bearing. And if you bought a part from someone who called it "slightly warm," the pamphlet about that is by the door. Read it outdoors. — K.
