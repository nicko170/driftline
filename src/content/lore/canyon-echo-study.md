## Why glass talks back

The canyon was made by eleven minutes of reactor fire, and its walls are fused smooth as a bell's inside — which is to say the whole run is a bell you ride through. Sound off bare stone scatters and dies; sound off glass comes home polished. A shout in the Glassroad returns four, five, six times, layered like a round being sung by increasingly tired versions of yourself.

Practical notes for the new rider: your engine note returns **delayed and doubled**, and on a fast pass it sounds like a rival on your tail. There is no rival. Do not brake to check. This is entry one in every rider's Glassroad education and the canyon administers it free of charge.

## Celebrated returns, as catalogued

**The Auction.** A Guild factor, losing a negotiation by radio, shouted the final insult as she entered the canyon and had it returned to her, in full, with interest, for the remainder of the run. The canyon is understood to have sided with the other party.

**The Proposal.** A courier rehearsed a marriage offer the length of the canyon, working on the wording. Somewhere near the S-curves the returns aligned, the wobbly lines fell out, and the canyon handed her one clean, complete, perfect version. She delivered it word for word. She is married. This is the only documented case of the canyon editing, and the Choir has Opinions.

**The Humming.** Riders who hum the cradle-band lullaby through the narrows report the walls joining in on the third bar *even when the radio is off*. Filed under: do not file.

## The early echo

Once in the log. A rookie, white-knuckling her first solo run, called ahead — the standard joke — *"What's around the bend?"* The answer came back **before** her voice had finished leaving her: *"Nothing yet."*

She rode the rest in a silence so total that her own engine sounded apologetic, reached the far gate unharmed, and never said another word in that canyon for the rest of her career, which was long. Cantor Ilex's annotation, in teal ink: *A polite instrument does not interrupt. But an instrument that has been played for two hundred years learns the song. Be kind with what you sing into it.*

## Riding guidance

Keep calls short at speed — long shouts smear and return as false traffic. Enjoy yourself at walking pace, where the canyon is generous and the rounds are beautiful. And if the echo answers early: it already told you the important part. Keep riding.
