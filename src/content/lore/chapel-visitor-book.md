*Exhibit, Salt Guild Archive (on indefinite loan to the mid-span chapel, whose keepers refuse to say whose). Transcriptions by the current keeper.*

## The Plaque (bolted to the chapel's door frame, brass, long since gone teal)

> THIS CHAPEL CROSSES NOTHING.
> IT CROSSES THE CROSSING.
> BUILT BY THE RIDERS OF THE FIRST WALL YEAR, WHO DID NOT ALL RIDE HOME.
> SIGN THE BOOK. STORMS COUNT NAMES.

## The Book (selected entries, across the years)

> *Year 4 of the Delay.* — "Three walls this season. Signed, alive, unreasonably. — I. Okafor."

> *Year 7.* — "Chapel roof held in the glass hail. Left a new lamp. Take what you need, leave what you can, tell the canyon your name so something knows it. — B. Espinal, westbound."

> *Year 19.* — "My turn to keep the chapel. Keeper Amadou has gone where keepers go; the book says his last storm was 'courteous.' I will aspire. My wife says the canyon echo is blasphemy. The canyon echo said it back in her exact voice. She has stopped objecting. — Keeper Vale."

> *Year 41.* — "Outran a wall with a wedding aboard. The couple signed before I did. Fair. — S. Quist."

> *Year 63.* — "The chapel door opens into the wind now. Rehung it myself. If it opens out of the wind again, the wind has changed and you should check the sky twice. — Keeper Sintara."

> *Year 88.* — "Rode the span in the season's first wall and the lamps were all lit before I got here. Nobody admits lighting them. Signed, grateful, and looking over my shoulder. — A. Reyes."

> *Year 100.* — "A hundred years of the Delay and the book is on its fourth volume. The first three are in the dry strongbox, shelf under the altar, if any keeper after me forgets. You won't forget. Keepers don't. — Keeper Osei."

> *Year 129.* — "My turn. The chapel wanted a reader, says the book. I read to the canyon now; the echo finishes the sentences. This is either devotion or madness and the distinction has never once seemed urgent. — Keeper Toll."

> *Year 158.* — "Lost half the convoy in the white wall. Signed for them all. If that breaks the rule, the storms can take it up with me next season. — R. Davenant, first run."

> *Year 160-something.* — "Bookkeeping of the soul: two hundred and eleven storms shelved by this door, two hundred and eleven names that outrode them. The strongest box in the building is not the strongbox. — Keeper Toll, sweeping."

## Keeper's Addendum (current)

New couriers: the pen is in the lamp drawer, the ink is in the second lamp drawer, and the third lamp drawer is a lamp. One entry per storm survived, no more, no fewer — the chapel keeps honest count even if you don't. Visitors who have faced no storms may sign the back page, which is labelled PENDING, and should not take this personally. The canyon reads every entry aloud at dusk. It is the only congregation that has never once missed a service.
