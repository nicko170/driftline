*Day-log of Lamp-Adjunct Sorrel Vey, third season in the Hollow. Copied for the archive with her spelling, her candle-smudges, and her opinions intact.*

## First Round — After Matins, Before Breakfast

Sixty-four lamps ring the crater path. First round wants the *short* ladder, the one with the rung Rill carved her name into the season before she went courier. Broad-Bench Odo carries it; he hums the Names while he walks so the lamps know who's coming. Lamps don't care. Odo knows lamps don't care. That's the point of Odo.

We light westward, against the sun, because Cantor Ilex says a flame should greet the light, never chase it. The Choir argues about this twice a year, warmly, like family.

## Noon Round — The Difficult One

Lamp Forty-One stands alone off the path, down where the crater floor starts thinking about being the crater floor. Nobody agrees on who built it. The listening horn moans through it when the wind sits just so, and the flame will not stay lit unless the lighter *says something true*.

The Adjunct roster treats Forty-One as a hazing; the Cantor treats it as a sacrament; both are the same event seen from different ends of the ladder. Little Reverb lit it first try, aged eight, by telling it she was scared of the dark. It has stayed lit for her ever since. It has also — and the log records this neutrally — stayed lit for traders, a lost goat, and one Guild auditor, whose truth is sealed.

What I told it is sealed too. Find me at dinner.

## Dusk Round — Voicework

After dusk the crater stops behaving like geography and starts behaving like company. Words said near the horn come back a beat later, *almost* the same. The congregation calls it the Reply. The engineers the Reclaimers send call it constructive interference, and then they stand there listening for an hour like everyone else.

Rule of the dusk round: light the ring, say the Name you carry, then say nothing you'd be ashamed to hear tomorrow in a kinder voice. The Reply has never once repeated a cruelty kindly. It repeats *kindness* exactly. I have stopped believing this is acoustics. The Cantor has stopped asking me to.

## Midnight Notes

Sixty-four lit, one re-lit, zero lost. The horn moaned a fifth tonight — low, patient, *recent*, like something that has started practising. Odo says the sky is tuning. I wrote it down because writing is what the ladder-carriers do when the dark hums back.

— S.V., Lamp-Adjunct, the Hollow
