*Compiled by Boss Pyke from the Verdantine Combine's original manual (unrecoverable, eaten politely) and forty years of the paper log. Union Local 9 abridgement. Where the original said SAFETY MARGIN, this edition says the margin was measured once by a woman with grease in her eyebrows and found to be "about this much," and we have kept her measurement. It has never been wrong. That is the entire quality system.*

---

## Section 1: Starting the unit

Chock the drum. Clear the chute. Hum first. *(Original manual: "engage the drive clutch." We find humming first engages the clutch better. — P.)* If the unit hums back before you finish the first bar, cancel the shift and check the feed plate for butterscotch, gloves, wedding tokens, or couriers. All four have precedent.

## Section 2: Margins

Redacted for speed. *(This is the joke the new hands are required to pretend is a joke. The margins are written on the wall of the sorting shed in chalk, and the chalk is refreshed every storm season, and nobody photographs it, because some load-bearing things should only exist where the crew stands. — P.)*

## Section 3: Rule Two

**Rule Two: if it jams and then unjams itself, stop being curious.** The unit has a reverse cycle it does not mechanically have. This has been true since Year 19 and was true once before Year 1, which is the entry we do not discuss. Do not log the unjamming as a malfunction. Log it as the unit *declining*, and apologise to whatever you fed it.

## Section 4: Aunt Vertex's addenda (binding)

*[In Vertex's hand, in a pencil that smells of thunder:]*

ONE. The crusher and I have an arrangement: it does not get bored near my powder and I do not store powder within its daydreaming radius. Honour our arrangement. TWO. If the drum hums the pull-song's *third* verse unasked, evacuate upslope and send for me — that is the note it sang the night Anchor Seven went up in a light the sky remembers, and I would like to be present for the sequel. THREE. Casserole bakes on the north housing only. The south housing runs eight degrees cooler and the unit keeps it that way *for a reason*, and that reason is mine.

## Section 5: Not for crushing (standing list, abridged)

Choir lamp-glass. Anything still warm. Anything that was *ever* warm in the personal sense. Skyship keels under moorage lien. The weather goat. Items accompanied by a courier who says "it's fine" — the courier is wrong, take the item gently and bill the Guild. Doors. **Any** door, of **any** size, regardless of who asks — see the bench notes on wake-keys, and see also the boss's ruling, which is underlined hard enough to emboss: *you don't crush a knock, sprout.*

Manifests marked NULL. The fee schedule for crushing a null manifest is listed as "see me" and "me" is underlined twice.

Still operational. Operate accordingly.
