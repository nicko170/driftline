*The creed says the Driftline does not open the mail. It says nothing about the mail the Driftline writes to itself, for free, out of pure loneliness and spite. Every storm shelter has a bottle under the third bunk. Every well-kept cairn has a wax tube under the capstone. And the moorage mast in Saltmouth has a board that constitutes, by any honest measure, the desert's longest-running newspaper. Selections follow, transcribed before the wind finished its edit. — transcription circle*

## From the shelter bottles

**"Wall came through at dusk, season's eleventh. Shelter held. Ate your biscuits, whoever you are. Left you two spark plugs and an apology, which is worth more."** — *unsigned, Bitter Flats shelter*

**"To the courier who tightened my cargo strap at the relay fire and said nothing: I noticed. The crate was my marriage. It holds. So do I, mostly."** — *unsigned, Drowned Array shelter*

**"R.D. was here. Took nothing, left the kettle full, which is the whole religion in one gesture."** — *initialed K., relay shelter north of the Glassroad. The circle notes the handwriting matches the maintenance wall.*

**"If you found the storm-mole mound by the door, DO NOT report it to the weather desk. They will send a form. The goat already knows."** — *P., stamped with a hoof print of contested authenticity*

## From the cairn tubes

**"Third cairn past the Last Lamp: the slick bend is slicker. I have done my duty. The next fool is warned."** Tradition holds that reading a cairn slip obliges you to add one. It does not oblige you to be right, which is why half the tubes are arguments:

**"The bend is fine. Learn to drift."** — *J.M.*

**"The bend is NOT fine. I learned to drift. That's what saved me."** — *Q.K., in the green ink she is famous for*

**"You are both banned from my cairn."** — *the cairnkeeper, presumably*

## From the moorage-mast board, Saltmouth

**FOUND:** one horizon cell, dented, humming faintly. **LOST:** my patience at the Guild window. If you have either, I am the tall one by the soup crate. *(The circle notes the patient was returned. The horizon cell was kept as evidence.)*

**WANTED:** somebody to tell Theo's radio that nobody is on the other end. It keeps asking. It deserves a kind answer. *(See also: every courier who has ever carried the messages-for-nobody round and answered it themselves. You know who you are.)*

**NOTICE, posted by the tower keeper in a slow and beautiful hand:** THE CUPS ARE COUNTED. THE CUPS HAVE ALWAYS BEEN COUNTED. — *M.A.*

**Under it, in chalk, in at least nine hands:** we know. *(One hand, later, smaller: thank you.)*

## The one rule

Notes in bottles are not mail. They carry no postage, no flag, no guarantee, and they are the only words on Kessa-9 that travel at exactly the speed of friendship. Leave the bottle drier than you found it. Leave the next courier something worth braking for.

*Flat salt, full pan, following wind.*
