*Compiled in the back pages of the Saltmouth job-board ledger by generations of couriers who were supposed to be signing in. Entries in courier hand; corrections in Ketch's; annotations in a third hand everyone has agreed not to identify (it is Old Bahro).*

## The Essentials

**Glassed** — stranded mid-canyon with a dead cell and excellent acoustics for regretting your life. *"Toll sang to me while I was glassed. Took the edge off. Billed nobody."*

**Dusted** — outrun your own dust plume at speed; a compliment. *"She dusted herself on the Slick Mile"* means the witness became briefly spiritual.

**Wax-blind** — unable to read a manifest because someone sealed it over the address. Extremely common. Never the Guild's fault. Always the Guild's fault.

**A Ketch** — (noun) a repair done with the wrong part, done properly. *"The whole fairing's a Ketch."* — Counter-entry in Ketch's hand: *ASSUMED A COMPLIMENT. WILL BEHAVE ACCORDINGLY.*

**Riding the hum** — following engine resonance so clean the bike stops feeling mechanical and starts feeling borrowed. Origin unknown, and half the ledger insists the term predates the engines. Do not tell the Choir couriers say this. We have enough letters in green ink.

## The Insults Section

**A Pemmy** — a forecast made with confidence by someone who cannot see weather. *See also: Guild weather desk.*

**Vane-foot** — fear of the ground, seasonal, incurable, expensive. Named by Keel Towners for their civic dockmaster, who has heard the term and filed an objection.

**Started a Brinn** — to pay over the odds for something because the seller was laughing. *"Forty credits for a spool of cable, but honestly? Started a Brinn."*
 — Annotation in the third hand: *"She once sold ME a rope. I was the rope's friend first. Fine sale."*

**Glass-rider** — a courier who only takes the pretty routes. Said with respect. Said, specifically, *at* people.

## The Tender Ones

**Flat salt** — farewell; *may your luck lie level*. Also a blessing, an apology, and — whispered — a prayer nobody's temple owns.

**Soup season** — any stretch of time in which couriers stop at benches. Supposedly a season. Actually a decision.

**The long way home** — a necessary error, ridden on purpose, at full maintenance cost, annually, forever. *See: Bahro's envelope; see also: why the Driftline exists when the Guild's maps say it shouldn't need to.*
