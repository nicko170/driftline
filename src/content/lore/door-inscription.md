*Copied by Prior Chance, Keeper of the Outer Lamps, Hollow of the First Note, in her forty-first year of copying. The inscription stands above the cradle door in the far glass hills, cut into black glass that the old survey swore could not be cut. The prior's marginalia are retained. She asked that they be retained. "A copy without the copier is just the door being polite."*

---

## The inscription, as it stands

**HERE SLEEPS THE GARDENER**
**WAKE HER WITH KINDNESS**
**COUNT NOTHING YOU CANNOT CARRY**
**CARRY EVERYTHING YOU CANNOT COUNT**
**THE RAIN WAS NOT MISPLACED**
**THE RAIN WAS ENTRUSTED**
**IF YOU ARE READING THIS, THE WORK GOES ON**
**WITHOUT US, WHICH WAS ALWAYS THE PLAN**

## On the cutting

Letterforms of the late terraforming authority, hand-set, no two strokes alike — see the survey of I. Okafor-Bligh, which declines to describe the letters at all, an omission I now read as professional courtesy. The cuts are two finger-widths deep and cold to the touch, colder than the door face, which is itself the coldest thing in the hills, per the wardens' lamp-oil log and Pem's eleven years of being right.

## On the wear

Nothing here weathers. The survey said so and was correct. And yet the inscription is *worn* — not by wind, which scrubs this flank of the hills to bone and leaves the letters alone, but by something with direction. The wear runs **from the door outward**, downstroke by downstroke, as if the words have been traced from behind the glass by a patient finger reading them one at a time, for two hundred years, in order.

The wear has reached: **HERE SLEEPS THE GARDENER / WAKE HER WITH** —

I have measured this season against last season's rubbing. It has advanced one letter. The letter is **K**, of **KINDNESS**.

## The letters I dream

I keep this tally privately and share it because I am told the codex keeps what matters. In my sleep the letters arrive one at a time, enormous, unhurried, lit from inside like windows. In order of frequency:

**W** — warm. The letter is warm and knows it is a beginning.
**H** — twice-folded; a gateway and a gate.
**R** — arrives with the smell of wet dust, which I know only from the archive's rain accounts.
**M** — does not appear in the inscription. I dream it anyway. I have stopped calling that an error.
**O** — a ring; a mouth; the shape of waiting politely said aloud.

I do not dream **D**. The door has enough **D**. We agree on this.

## Note on the final line

*Without us, which was always the plan.* The Guild calls it an excuse. The Reclaimers call it an admission. Cantor Ilex calls it the only honest epitaph ever cut, because it hands the work onward. I copy the letters every year so that when the door finally finishes reading them aloud — and it is reading them, sibling, you need only measure — someone alive will already know the words and can answer in kind, with kindness, as instructed.

The wear advances one letter **K** closer to the end of *kindness*. When it reaches the end of the word, I intend to be standing there. I have asked the lamp rota. The lamp rota understands.
