Transcribed from the east wall of the Saltmouth garage, where it is repainted whenever the wind sands it down. Lettering: large. Additions: smaller. The additions are considered equally binding.

**THE DRIFTLINE CREED**

*Any crate. Any storm. Any door.*

*We carry what is given and we do not open it.*
*We are paid by the crossing, not the cargo.*
*The line holds because we hold it.*

*Flat salt and a following wind.*

— and beneath, in smaller paint, in roughly forty different hands:

*Article the first: you can carry anything once.*
*Article the second: the meter is a loan shark with excellent manners.*
*Article the third: brake plus steering is a drift, not a crash.*
*Article the fourth: never race the weather-goat's window. The goat knows.*
*Article the fifth: if the crate hums, hum back. (See Ketch re: dominance.)*
*Article the sixth: a delivered apology still counts as a delivery.*
*Article the seventh: the Choir hears, the Guild counts, the Reclaimers strip — and the Driftline carries. All four are necessary. Three will tell you otherwise.*
*Article the eighth: what you find on the flats between sunrise and second bell is yours to log and the Guild's to tax.*
*Article the ninth: water shared is water doubled. This one is just true.*
*Article the tenth, crossed out and repainted three times: the creed says nothing about what carries you back.*

There is a space beneath the tenth article, kept empty by unspoken agreement. Every few years someone paints a new line there and every few years the wind takes it off again before anyone agrees on the wording. Ketch says the wall types faster than the desert can argue. He also says the last line that survived a full season read: *the line holds because we hold it* — which is, of course, already in the creed.

The Guild has twice offered to buy the wall and relocate it to a museum in Keel Town. Both offers are painted on the wall, in the smallest letters of all, under the heading *ANY DOOR.*
