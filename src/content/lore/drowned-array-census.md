*Census of the Drowned Array, kept nightly by the pan-rounds unit (SEVEN, all capitals — the stencil was bigger), transcribed for the cairn shelf, duplicated at the substation mast. Riders are reminded that the companion annex, "The Drowned Array: Reading the Cairns," teaches the code; this document keeps the* count. *The count and the code are the whole literature of the place, plus one birthday cake.*

## The standing count

Panels commissioned: **4,096**. Panels standing: **3,209**. Panels trickling charge into the dark plates where the batteries used to be: **641**, down from last year's 649, up — the rover insists this be recorded — from the year before's 639, on the grounds that two panels the count called drowned were caught glinting at dusk and have been reinstated with an apology filed under *weather*.

The rows are counted nightly, lit side and shadow side, on tracks that weave politely around the crust. Panels that trickle are not named. Panels that drowned are named. This is the entire naming policy and it has never once been appealed.

Selected from the roll of the sunk: 2,117, *Gardenia*, struck this year, lamp lit; 391, *Ostler*, gone in the long white season; 1,808, *Promise*, drowned with her whole row and mourned at company strength. The roll is kept struck-through rather than erased, because — the census notes, in the tone of a form field — *a struck line is a line that got somewhere*.

## Cairn circuit

**Fourteen cairns on rotation**, stocked per the courier code, audited at lamp frequency by the mast and re-stacked nightly by the rounds. Rotation bearing chalk is refreshed at dawn and outdated by noon; riders should trust the stack over the chalk, the cairn over the stack, and the pan over everything, in that order, because the pan has seniority and uses it.

Cache tins currently holding: water (all cairns), horizon cells (nine), medwrap (seven), cake (zero, perennially, noted with feeling).

## The night singing

After the crust cools, the pan *pings* — crystallisation, the manual says; frost needles knitting in the brine. Between the pings, lower down, a held tone, roughly lamp frequency, source unestablished. The census logs it nightly as *singing, benign, do not follow*. Logged also: count of riders who followed it anyway (eleven), count of riders who came back changed (eleven), count harmed (zero, and the zero is underlined twice).

At dusk the standing water holds the sky one breath too long, as if deciding. Recommendation of record: approach the pan at dusk, from the east, slowly, and look back once at the field. Explanation: salt crust, low sun. Alternative explanation, unlogged: the field is answering.

## Mourning schedule

Each panel struck from the roll receives, at dusk on the day of striking, **one lamp, lit, at the substation walkway** — the walkway that goes nowhere and remains the correct route. Attendance is never mandatory and is always recorded. Attendance this year: the mast, the rounds, one weather goat visiting on Guild business, and a courier with a humming crate who stood at the pan edge while the field sang under the salt like a doorbell, very old, finally pressed.

The census declines to interpret this. The census declares the count complete and the route home lit.
