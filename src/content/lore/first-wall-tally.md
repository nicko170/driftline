*The board is two metres of blackened timber, rescued from the old water tower and hung where the coffee steam can reach it. Rain-warped, chalk-scuffed, and never once re-painted. It is the only archive the Driftline keeps that the Guild has not tried to charge rent on.*

---

## The custom

Ride your first storm wall alone, come back, chalk your name. Three columns: **NAME. WALL. WORD.** The word is whatever the wall said to you, or whatever you said back, and you may not borrow somebody else's. Pem maintains the chalk tin and enforces the borrowing rule with the patience of a bailiff.

Nobody records whether they were scared. The board assumes it. That's the courtesy.

## Entries, as they stand

KESSA BRINE. *Brass-coloured, low fast one.* Word: **finally.**

MARROW, T. (Senior). *Grey. Taller than the mesa.* Word: **small.**

DAX [surname worn away]. *The year-thirty-one knock.* Word: **accurate.** *(Margin note in another hand: of course.)*

VASKA. *Copper. Smelled of rain that never fell.* Word: **again.** *(First word ever borrowed twice on the board; Pem oiled the tin and allowed it, once, because the second VASKA was the first one's daughter, and the wall that took the mother was the wall the daughter rode through.)*

DAVENANT, R. *Black. Windspine high.* Word: **extra.** *(The usual note under this one — six hands, all saying the same thing: explain. Nobody has. The chalk has been refreshed more times than any other entry, and the word is always written back the same: extra, like the wall owed her and she meant to collect.)*

KETCH [no surname, one bolt drawn where it would go]. *Rust-red, flat light.* Word: **repaired.**

PEM. *Year of the Two Walls doing it wrong.* Word: **borrowed.** *(This is technically a rule violation. Pem wrote it in Pem's own tin-chalk and dares you.)*

## The poem

Read the word column aloud at season turn, top to bottom, oldest first: *finally small accurate again extra repaired borrowed...* It runs on for two hundred entries. Old hands claim the last word of any season predicts the storms of the next one, and that the season Junior Kech chalked **hungry**, half the ridge choked. The new riders laugh. Then they check the last word before they accept a night run.

## The space at the bottom

There's a clean square at the board's bottom right, no older than spring. The chalk in the tin is fresh. The coffee urn below is hot.

Word pending.
