*Collected and chalked fair from the gate posts of the Glassroad span, by order of audit season. Each ruling stands where it was first ruled, in chalk, on the post concerned; this sheet is the courteous copy. Annotations in the smaller hand are the Warden's. Main clauses rule. Margin clauses are true.*

## Ruling 114 — On payment in song

A convoy of eleven haulers, short of scrip at mid-span, offered toll in song: three verses, harmony optional, delivered standing. **Accepted at face value.** — *A song is a receipt the road can hum back. I have since heard the second verse in the canyon at dusk, returned improved. No Guild instrument does this.*

## Ruling 117 — On payment in apology

One solo rider, purse lost to the dunes, offered apology: sincere in parts, rehearsed in others. **Accepted, provisionally, pending sincerity.** Sincerity to be determined by the echo. — *The echo repeats insincerity in a smaller voice. It has never once been wrong about this. The rider's apology grew more sincere mid-span and was upgraded to VALID at the chapel.*

## Ruling 122 — On exact change

A traveller tendered exact change, counted twice, to the granule. **Suspected.** Toll accepted; suspicion chalked for the record. — *Every honest traveller arrives altered by the crossing and owes the road its difference. Exact change pretends the road changes nothing. The manuscript dislikes pretense. So, for the record, do I.*

## Ruling 128 — On double payment

A party of two paid the toll twice and declined to explain. **Chalked as piety. Non-refundable.** — *Piety is the only category of freight the span does not weigh. If they were paying for someone who could not cross, the second toll has been banked against that crossing, forever, interest accruing in the road's favour.*

## Standing precedent

The gate is not a shop. The manuscript collects *proof of passage*, not profit: a song, an apology, a coin, a true story — anything that testifies the traveller arrived on purpose. Rides charged per ego, not axle; Freewarden crossings exempt, always; the keeper crosses free and pays in keeping.

*On the Warden's own door, the tally has never balanced. This is conceded annually, entered as ROUNDING, and forgiven where found.*

## Guild annotation

*Stamped below in Chapterhouse ochre, twice, slightly crooked:*

**AUDITED. UNORTHODOX BUT AIRTIGHT.** Variance totals one (1) song, one (1) apology, one (1) unexplained second payment. The Guild agrees not to invoice the precedent, the lead being: *the Guild does not charge the road for being a road. The Guild is not afraid of the road. The Guild simply no longer audits it.*
