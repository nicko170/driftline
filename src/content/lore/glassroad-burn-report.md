*INCIDENT REPORT 7-K — prepared by M. Yao, site engineer (retained), Canyon Array Station 4. Classification: COMPANY-SAD. That is not a real classification. There is no one left to tell us what is real.*

---

## The event

Reactor vessel K-7 — "the Kettle" — was slated for defuel and burial per wind-down protocol. During defuel, containment cooling failed across seventeen of eighteen loops. The eighteenth loop worked the hardest of its life. Vessel temperature exceeded casing rating at 02:14 local. Rather than breach the array, we cut the Kettle loose down the discharge canyon, which we had dug for water that never came.

**(Marginal note, later hand: "You surfed a dying reactor down a dry riverbed." Yes. You weren't there. There were two of us. One of us held the latch. The other wrote this.)**

**Course.** The vessel travelled 9.4 kilometres down-canyon on its own heat, melting the walls to slag as it went. Estimated core temp at burial: don't measure things that hot with the tools available to people who have quit being paid. It came to rest at the canyon mouth and is, per latest sounding, still down there, still faintly warm. Saying so out loud makes the Choir hum and the Reclaimers reach for their spanners, so we say it in reports only.

**Result.** The canyon floor, walls and half its moraine fused to glass — teal where the melt ran thick, white where it flashed. Depth of melt varies. Cleavage flat. Ripple grain follows the old watercourse. It is the single smoothest 9.4 kilometres on the planet and it runs exactly between the two places everyone needs to get between. This was not a design.

**Vein heat, two centuries on.** The thick seams hold thermal charge from the sun and from below. Veins read hot at noon, warm at midnight. Birds nest over them in winter. We did not plan the birds either.

**Recommendations.** 1. Do not attempt salvage of K-7. 2. Mark the canyon impassable. 3. *(struck through, same hand:)* There is a courier outside who rode the length of it in nine minutes and wants to know what the record is. Recommendation 2 is withdrawn. The road made itself. Send cones.

*Filed, unread, unfiled, found, and read constantly. — annex copy, Saltmouth archive*
