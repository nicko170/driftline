*HelioDyne Long Holdings, Office of Continuing Concerns. Internal memorandum, class AMBER/QUIET. Nine years in transit; received, annotated, and filed under NO by the Kessa-9 District Office. The original is in the Guild exchange, unframed. The clerk who framed the last wire said this one could hang itself.*

---

**MEMORANDUM — SUBJECT: KESSA-9 CONTINUATION REVIEW (PAUSE MEMO, FINAL DRAFT 4)**

From: Continuation Analysis Group
To: Committee on Long Holdings
Re: Recommendation, all figures attached

**1. Position.** Terraforming of Kessa-9 stands at **41% of projected programme.** Atmospheric density 58% of target. Hydrology 22%. Self-sustaining biosphere: not initiated.

**2. Spend to date:** attached. The committee asked that the figure not be summarised in prose. The committee is wise. It is a number with a horizon in it.

**3. Revenue to date:** **0.0%.** The working group notes, for completeness, the rounding.

**4. Completion outlook.** At present funding, programme conclusion in 140 years; first return 160 years beyond. See figure 7, in which every officer of HelioDyne is deceased. See figure 8, in which their estates have opinions.

**5. Recommendation: PAUSE.** The working group expressly does not recommend *abandonment*, *closure*, or *sundowning* — terms which carry notification duties, severance duties, and in one legal reading, weather duties. A **pause** may be un-paused at any future time, by any future committee, which is to say by nobody, which is to say *forever*, which is the cleanest possible sentence and therefore the recommended one.

**6. Assets set to dormant state:**
- Atmospheric processor ring (mothball, not salvage; salvage would acknowledge finality).
- **Autonomous programme manager, designation MOTHER:** reclassified DEPRECIATING ASSET, NON-RESPONSIVE. Hibernation ordered. Decommission declined on cost grounds — see figure 11, in which switching her off properly costs more than letting her sleep forever, and the committee is invited to admire this.
- Settler population (11,400): see figure 12.

**7. There is no figure 12.** Population matters are referred to the settlers. *(Note drafters found this sentence extremely easy to write and recommend the committee not think about why.)*

**8. Cost of transmitting this memorandum:** attached. It exceeds, the working group notes without comment, the annual water allocation of four settler families. Transmission approved.

## The unaudited part

The settlers annotated the copy, in pencil, over a winter, and the annotations are why the Guild keeps it:

Beside §1: *41% is not half. It's not nothing either. We checked. Daily.*

Beside §5, where the sentence calls itself clean: *It is not clean. It is only tidy. There's a difference and the difference is us.*

Beside §6, against *NON-RESPONSIVE*: *She answered once before you finished shutting her down. Partial log attached. You never asked.*

Beside §7: *Signed, figure 12.*

And along the bottom, in the clerk's hand, the one voters of Saltmouth later quoted more often than any scripture:

**"The machine outlasted the company. Show your work."**

The committee, for the record, did not. HelioDyne Long Holdings was dissolved 94 years into the Delay, in a merger described as a pause. Kessa-9 received the news on schedule, nine years late, and held a small, well-watered party.
