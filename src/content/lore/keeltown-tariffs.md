*Posted sheet, Keel Town west rim, frame glass long since blown out. Printed text in bold; everything else is the two centuries talking. Transcribed in full, as posted, because tearing it down was proposed in 214 and the whole town voted with wrenches. — Ledger Office, Keel Town branch*

---

## SALT GUILD — Moorage Tariffs, Keel Town
*per ship, per day, per witness*

**KEEL, freight class:** 40 credits.
*(in the margin, ochre ink: "Was 35." beneath: "Was NEVER 35." beneath, tiny: "was 35 once, I was there" — D.)*

**GULL, courier-rigged:** 12 credits, storms at dockmaster's mercy.
*(pencil, large: "THE MERCY IS ALSO TWELVE CREDITS." initialled, with exaggerated politeness, by three different dockmasters.)*

**TENDER, private:** 8 credits. **Do not call them boats on this sheet.**
*(someone has underlined the sentence so hard the fibre tore.)*

**BEREAVEMENT LANTERN:** no fee. *No fee. No fee.* The printed sheet only says it once. The margins keep saying it.

**WINCH, per load:** 2 credits, base to rim. 3 credits if the load complains.
*(annotation: "loads complained four times. all four were goats. billed anyway." — G.)*

**STORAGE, overnight:** 5 credits. **STORAGE, overnight, volatile:** 15 and a written apology in advance.
*(margin: "the apology is filed under 'storage, combustible.' standard form. ask for Form 22-B." another hand: "ask for 22-B at your own risk, they have SO many.")*

**GUILD LOAN, hull-secured:** rates posted at the factor desk.
*(oldest ink on the sheet, rust-brown, almost gone: "a debt that never closes. ask me how I know." — unsigned. later hand: "everyone knows. that's the signature.")*

**STORY, witnessed, in lieu:** one shift in ten, per Article Five. Valuation by clerk.
**(clerk's note, recent, amber: "narrative dividends up. the desert is talkative. drink your water.")**

**COURIER BERTH, driftline plate shown:** waived.
*(margin, ochre: "who waived this." answer, amber, confident: "the Driftline did. file a complaint with the wind." tallies below it run to forty-one. the dispute is generational.)*

**FEE, UNLISTED, ITEM 14:** don't ask.

*(a catalogue of hands have asked anyway, in increasing desperation, down the margin and onto the frame: "asked what." "IT." "what is IT." "you will KNOW when it is TIME." "that is not accounting." "no. it is fourteen.")*

**LAST LINE, MANDATORY TO PRINT:** The docks belong to the Guild. The wind does not. File complaints accordingly.

*(someone has added, in salt-white paint taller than the sheet: "COMPLAINTS 2 CREDITS, WIND FILES THEM FOR FREE." it has been varnished over to preserve it.)*
