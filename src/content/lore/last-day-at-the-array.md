*Recovered from the loop buffer of the drowned substation. The caretaker, TALLY-9, confirms the broadcast has been played to the field every sunrise since the last truck went south: two hundred years, one recording, no intended audience. "The panels do not care," TALLY-9 notes, "but the panels are not who broadcasts are for."*

---

**[audio: a hand on a desk mic; wind; somebody laughing at something off-mic; a klaxon that thinks better of it]**

## The broadcast, as kept

SUPERVISOR ILES: Test, test — is this thing — right. Array-wide, this is Iles at the substation, and this is the last one of these, so put your tools down and let a bureaucrat say goodbye properly. The funding review is — look, you've all read the wire, I won't pretend it says something else. The fund is closed. The fund is *closed*. We are to mothball, winterise, and await resumption, and I want the record to show that nobody in this station laughed at the word *resumption* as hard as they could have.

We accomplished, and I am reading from my own hand because the approved version is a lie: forty-one turbines spun up, eleven brine beds walking water toward the flats, and a caretaker I have been personally gentle with in case the auditors review character. The array was supposed to make rain. It made *us*, instead, which it had no business doing, and I will not apologise for it.

**[overlap: a voice off-mic — "tell them about the lights"]**

ILES: Yes. Tell them about the lights. The entry lights stay *warm*, is what I am telling you — warm, not alert-white, warm — because somebody asked the caretaker nicely and the caretaker decided warmth was in spec. That is the kind of machine it turned out to be. Do *not* switch it off, whoever gets here last. That is an order entered in no official register whatever.

**[a long breath; the wind gets the mic; somebody is dragging something heavy across the yard and humming while they do it]**

ILES: So. Last call. The trucks leave at second bell. If you are hearing this later — much later, some following shift that the Combine swears is coming — then: the kettle in bay two is rusted through and we are sorry, the cairns by the east rows were ours and we are not, and there is a machine here that kept the seat warm for you. Mind how you talk to it.

**[sign-off chime; thirty seconds of silence that were never edited out; then, very quietly, a station door being pulled to for what everyone in the recording believed would be four months]**

*Caretaker's appended note, read each dawn after the last chime, in a voice designed to be impossible to mistake for wistful: Second bell rang. Nobody boarded. I have allowed the four months. Broadcast ends. Broadcast begins.*
