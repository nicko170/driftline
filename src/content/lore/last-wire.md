*Archive: Terraforming Authority, Kessa-9 District. File 0-0-1, "CLOSURE." Transcribed from the Salt Guild's copy of a copy; original notation preserved.*

## From the Final Budget Hearing (Earth, Committee on Long Holdings, year 0 of the Delay)

The hearing ran eleven minutes. This is known because the stenographer billed for twelve and was audited.

**CHAIR:** Item nine. Kessa-9 continuation funding. The rapporteur.

**RAPPORTEUR:** Honourable committee, the projected cost of completing Kessa-9's atmosphere and hydrology is, at present spend, one hundred and forty more years. The projected *return* begins one hundred and sixty years after that. The committee will note that every member of this committee will be dead before either event. The committee may further note that the committee's *grandchildren* —

**CHAIR:** The rapporteur will confine herself to figures.

**RAPPORTEUR:** Confining. The figure is zero. The recommendation is closure. *[inaudible]*

**MEMBER ARROYO:** For the record — what happens to the people already there?

**RAPPORTEUR:** Feed-lag means the question will reach them in a little over nine years. The answer arrives with it. They will have time to —

**CHAIR:** The member will confine himself to figures also.

**MEMBER ARROYO:** Then my figure is one. One additional year of operating funds, so they can wind down properly, tell the machine to sleep, tell the settlers —

**CHAIR:** Noted and declined. Item ten. *[the remainder of the hearing concerns maritime insurance]*

## The Last Wire (receiving record, Kessa-9 District Office)

**FUNDS TRANSFER — FINAL.**
Amount: 11 months operating reserve, per amended closure order (the one year requested, prorated).
Routing: Authority Long Holdings → Relay 4 → Relay 4 → Relay 4 *(latency surcharge deducted)* → Kessa-9 District.
Purpose field, per form: CLOSURE/DRAWDOWN.

Annotation, handwritten, by the receiving clerk, in the margin where purpose fields do not extend:

> *"(sorry)."*

The annotation is not part of the official record. It is also the only part of the official record the Guild chose to keep framed. Visitors to the exchange can see it. The frame cost more than the wire was ever going to be worth, which the Guild describes as the point.

Addendum, by Guild auditors of later years: the eleven months of operating reserve paid, in the event, four months of shutdown costs and, after that, the first year of the water charter that became Saltmouth. The money ran out. The people didn't. Historians are divided on whether this was the Authority's failure or the Authority's last gift by accident. The clerk's annotation disagrees with "accident."
