**SALT GUILD — DEEP ARCHIVE — FORM 7-C (CUSTODY, PERPETUAL). COPY 4 OF 1.**

*Filed by direction of Guildmaster O. Counter-signed by a machine that is not supposed to counter-sign.*

Article offered for perpetual custody: one (1) courier crate, manifest status *stray*. The archive defines a stray as cargo without provenance, and maintains a Ledger of Strays for such items. The Ledger of Strays is a real book. Rule: when a stray is shelved, its line in the Strays book is struck through and re-entered in the master ledger, because a thing that is archived has stopped being a mystery and started being *furniture*.

The crate was couriered to the archive stair on the nineteenth of Stormseason. The vault keeper reports the following, verbatim, under advice of counsel: "I opened the door to put the stray inside, and the stray did not wish to go inside, and I know how that sounds, thank you." The keeper has been granted leave and a modest raise.

Weight at intake: declined to be weighed. Sister Counterweight attended with the ceremonial scale. The scale's arm pointed, for eleven minutes, at *the courier*.

Disposition: custody declined by the archive, on grounds newly invented for the occasion — *the article appears to regard itself as in transit, and the archive holds only things that have stopped moving.*

Guildmaster's marginalia, in ochre ink: **"Then it shall keep moving until it tires. Engage the courier. Count it twice."**

Note from the night clerk: the Strays book now contains, on a fresh page, in ink that was dry before anyone touched the pen, a full manifest line for the crate — origin *Kessa-9*, sender *max kestrel authority terraforming engine, retired*, recipient *you know who you are*. The name field, when read aloud, causes the lamps to dim in appreciation.
