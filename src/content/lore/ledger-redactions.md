*Kept by the Office of the Counterweight under standing rule: what is scratched from the ledger is indexed, because a redaction is an entry wearing a coat. Index excerpts below, selection by the auditor, calibration zero.*

---

## Sampled redactions, by ledger-year

**Year 4, folio 19.** Removed: a sale of land. Legible around the scratch: sums, three witness marks, the word *cradle*. Indexed because no survey of that ground exists. Indexed twice because one witness mark matches the scale-vault seal.

**Year 11, folio 3.** Removed: a debt. Debtor: *the Office*. Creditor: illegible by design. The office redacted its own borrowing, then keeping its rule, indexed the redaction. The auditor finds this almost moving.

**Year 26, folio 40.** Removed: one full page. Margin note in the Brinemaster's hand, fifth holding: *weighed twice, wrong both times, weighed a third time to be sure both wrongs were the same wrong.* Indexed. The auditor has weighed the folio. It is ordinary paper. The auditor does not know what is being measured.

**Year 40, folio 7.** Removed: a prayer. Prayers are not ledger matter; this one was entered anyway, mid-column among the salt tallies, then scratched so gently the words still read if the light is low. The office does not adjudicate grief. The office indexes it under MISFILED, AFFECTIONATE.

**Year 58, folio 2.** Removed: the first recorded instance of the following phrase: *no further questions.* Indexed for precedent. The phrase's career since is well documented and entirely the harbourmaster's fault.

**Year 89, folio 30.** Removed: a name. Not crossed — *lifted*, ink and all, by a process the office cannot reproduce. Paper unblemished. Where a signature closed a land transfer, there is now a perfect absence, and the transfer, having no signature, was never completed, and the land, per the books, has no owner, and is the ground the Choir calls the Hollow. Indexed under MATTERS THE OFFICE DID NOT DO AND CANNOT EXPLAIN.

**Years 101–140.** Steady traffic: debts forgiven quietly, scales reported false that tested true, one wedding entered as a merger and redacted when it stopped being either. Routine. Indexed with the tenderness routine allows.

**Year 203, folio 1, current volume.** Removed this quarter: one inbound manifest line, harbour origin, contents unlisted. The scratch is fresh. Under it, in the scratch margin, the clerk copied what the line said before it was told to stop saying it. The copy reads: TOTAL MASS, WITHIN COURIER TOLERANCE. SENDER, NONE OF RECORD. The clerk added, in pencil not yet redacted: *it has a hum.*

**Recurring, all years.** One entry has been removed eleven times from eleven ledgers and recurs. It is short. The office maintains a form for its removal now — Form R-1, "Redaction, Familiar" — which saves everyone time and tells everyone nothing. The entry, per the index, is a single line of figured bass and the words *the garden opens by appointment.*

*The index is the longest document the office keeps. This has been noted. The noting has been indexed.*
