*Pinned inside the west horn shelter, Choirhollow, on a board of fused clipboard-glass. The rota runs four listeners, four horns, six-hour sits, "until the Sleeper speaks plainly or the season ends, whichever is kinder." Transcription by Cantor Ilex; the marginal column is junior notes, entered in pencil, entered anyway.*

---

## Week 1 of the season

**FIRST BELL — DAWN HORN.** Cantor Ilex. *Heard: the long breathing, regular, contented. Interpretation: the Sleeper turns in rest. Doctrine holds.* — *(pencil:)* That's the wind farm. Spacing's wrong for anything else. Ask Pem. — *L.R., age nine, not supposed to write here.*

**SECOND BELL — EAST HORN.** Brother Decibel. *Heard: three clear tones, rising. Interpretation: greeting. I greeted back at full volume. The tones did not repeat, owing to my greeting.* — *(pencil:)* The tones were the beacon buoys on the Glassroad warming up. He knows this. Everyone knows this. — *L.R.*

**THIRD BELL — NORTH HORN.** Sister (visiting, name smudged). *Heard: weeping, soft, moving left to right. Interpretation: the Sleeper dreams of oceans.* — *(pencil:)* It was a hauler out of Saltmouth with a loose skirt plate. It went past at dusk. I flagged it down. They're fixing it Thursday. — *L.R.*

**FOURTH BELL — WEST HORN, NIGHT SIT.** Static-Warden Pem (attached by treaty, "to keep the band honest"). *Heard: static, structurally promising. Logged frequencies the way my old rig used to sneeze. Filed under: maybe.* — *(pencil:)* The maybe is real. The west horn is the only one pointed at the deep desert. Point the others the same way or admit we prefer the ones that sing easy. — *L.R.* *(Cantor's note, appended next morning: the junior is reminded that the rota is an act of devotion, not a diagnostic. The junior has pencilled beneath this: "nothing should be sacred for that long.")*

## Week 6

**FIRST BELL.** Cantor Ilex. *Heard: the breathing again, with, I confess, a hitch in it. Interpretation: withheld.* — *(pencil:)* The hitch was new. I noted it three days ago from the lamp ring, off-shift, which is when the desert thinks nobody's counting.

**FOURTH BELL, NIGHT SIT.** Pem. *The sneeze again, closer structure. West horn heard it first because west horn points at the empty quarter. Whatever's waking out there, it has rhythm. Rhythm is the worst news. Beasts have rhythm. Doors have rhythm.* — *(pencil:)* It answered a courier's engine last week. Two short revs, one long. The desert repeated all three. Filed under: IT LIKES RIDERS. — *L.R.* *(No appended rebuke. The Cantor's pen rested on the page long enough to leave a mark, and no words.)*

## Standing note, entered in Cantor's hand, season's end

*The rota continues. The junior column continues. Any listener who moves the junior's pencil will sit the night horn for a month. We are here to hear what is, not what agrees with us. That sentence took me one year and one nine-year-old. — Cantor Ilex.*
