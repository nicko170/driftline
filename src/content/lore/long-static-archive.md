*Kept at the Hollow of the First Note in three binders, the third of which is just recipes. This index is copied to courier creed appendices because a rider who knows their static is a rider who knows when their radio is broken and when the desert simply has nothing to say. These are different emergencies.*

## The index (abridged, current to season 44)

**0.4 — "the cradle band."** Not dead. Plays the lullaby, has done for a hundred and ninety years, and is the reason this index exists at all: you cannot call a channel quiet until you have checked it against her son. *See: "Found Static: The Lullaby on the Dead Channel."*

**2.1 — "the cobbler."** Transmits, on no schedule anyone has caught, a sound like a hammer on a last. Eleven taps, pause, eleven taps. The Reclaimers claim it is a depth gauge on a buried pump, still doing its rounds. The Choir claims it is MOTHER mending a shoe for the desert. The Guild claims nothing but bills nobody, which for the Guild is a kind of worship.

**5.7 — "the widow's window."** Carrier wave only, steady as a held breath, aimed from an antenna on the boundary ridge at a spot of empty sky where a relay ship used to hold station. The ship is two centuries overdue. Somebody's descendent re-aims the antenna every time the wind takes it. Nobody has admitted to being the somebody in living memory, which means somebody is being very quiet about a very kind habit.

**9.9 — "the long argument."** Two automated buoys in the Drowned Array that have been exchanging the same twelve-word error message since before the Delay ended, each correcting the other, each wrong. Pem's note: *Do not feel bad for the buoys. They have never been interrupted. Many of us cannot say as much.*

**11.3 — "counting station."** Recites numbers in a trade creole nobody speaks fluently anymore. The numbers, when anyone bothers to plot them, are water levels. Somewhere below the pans, something is still minding the wells.

**14.0 — "the polite one."** Answers your radio check with an echo of your own call sign, four seconds late, slightly better pronounced. Nobody has found the source. Couriers consider it lucky. Rill Davenant considered it *nearly* lucky, and Rill's file is elsewhere.

## Keeper's standing note

A dead channel is not an empty one. It is a room with the light off, and this desert has more rooms than any of us has keys. Listen before you transmit. Log what you hear, even — *especially* — the boring silences. The boring silences are the ones that change.
