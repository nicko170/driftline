*Driftline Intake Form 1-A (Saltmouth office). Filed in duplicate; the duplicate is in worse shape, which the applicant is asked to believe means it was read more. Annotations in the hands of Ketch, garage keeper (gk), and Factor T. Cho, Salt Guild (tc). Original held under a solder spool in the garage office.*

---

## The Form

**FULL NAME:** Ash Varga. No middle name. *["There barely was a first," gk]*

**AGE:** Old enough to owe money. *["Checked. True, unfortunately," tc]*

**PREVIOUS EMPLOYMENT:** Brine shift, Saltmouth pans, two seasons. Loader-mate, Guild winch, one season, "dismissed for altitude reasons." *["Rode the cargo loop. Waved," tc. "Kid, that says FIRED," gk]*

**VEHICLE:** One hover-bike, make: disputed, model: "the Wren", condition: *optimistic*. *["Third-hand," gk, "and I don't count the bike's feelings, whatever that means." "Fourth-hand, by my ledger. Lien attached. 8,000 credits, Guild paper, signed with a flourish I did not enjoy," tc]*

**CAN YOU READ A SALTCLOCK?** Yes. *["Learned it in the pans, where the sun is your only manager and it is a bad one," gk]*

**HAVE YOU EVER BEEN LOST?** *["Applicant wrote 'no', crossed it out, wrote 'recovered'," tc. "Accepted," gk]*

**WHY THE DRIFTLINE?** *"Because everything out there is somebody's everything, and it sits there waiting, and somebody should take it. I'd like to be the somebody before the somebody who charges more."*

*["Read it twice," tc. "Half of it is insolence and half of it is the creed. I have filed it under both." "Kid means it," gk. "Worse luck for the kid."]*

**ATTITUDES TO SEALED CARGO:** Sealed means sealed. *["Underlined twice. Pressed so hard the page tore. Noted and, frankly, priced," tc]*

**REFERENCES:** The Brinemaster, who will describe me as "present". The winch crew, who will describe the altitude incident as "legendary" *["they will not," tc]* and nothing else.

---

## The Rulings

**DECISION OF KEEPER (gk):** *Teachable. Doesn't know it, which is the only kind worth the garage. Set them on the short runs. If they come back smiling, send them to Tamsin. If they come back not smiling, buy them soup and send them to Tamsin anyway — soup is cheaper than recruitment.*

**RULING OF FACTOR (tc):** *Insurable. Barely. Premium set at 'courier, unproven, mouthy'. The lien rides with them; the Guild likes its debts mobile. Approval stamped. Underline mine: this office takes no position on whether the Driftline is hiring a courier or the desert is hiring a witness.*

**APPLICANT'S ADDENDUM, discovered later, on the back:** *"You can stop reading here. I already left. — A.V."*

*["They had not left," tc. "They watched me read the back. They waved."]*
