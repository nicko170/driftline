*Transcribed by the Lamp Rest circle. The summit was held at Keel Town because neither faction would meet on the other's ground and neither could find neutral ground that wasn't technically a crime scene. — transcription circle*

**[theme: three notes, one flat, applause from three people]**

VELA MARLOWE: Evening, flats. Today: the crawler. Recap for the recently arrived — the season's first wall ate a Guild salt crawler out on the pan, and a Reclaimer crew under Boss Pyke was *winching it upright before the sand had finished settling*, which the Reclaimers call rescue and the Guild calls *theft with a helpful attitude*. Brinemaster Ogo called the Reclaimers "jackals with a union card." Pyke called the crawler "a hazard we have been paid, in exposure, to remove." Both statements were read into the record. Both records immediately disagreed.

So: summit. Guild brought manifest wax, Reclaimers brought the salvage ingot, and a courier carried *both* up the mesa to Dockmaster Devanna for arbitration — yes, the same courier, the one the storm couldn't name last week and apparently neither can I. *[static, apologetic]* Varga. I said Varga.

With me for post-mortem: Sister Counterweight for the Guild —

COUNTERWEIGHT: Everything crosses. Everything's counted. The crawler crossed. It has not been counted. That is the entire dispute.

MARLOWE: — and Aunt Vertex for the Reclaimers.

VERTEX: Salt that sits in a drowned crawler is just very wet masonry, dear. We *liberated* it. There's a whole hymn.

**[a hymn is not permitted to occur]**

MARLOWE: Ruling?

COUNTERWEIGHT: Pending. It is being counted twice, per custom.

VERTEX: Which means we'll appeal by morning, win by thaw, and retroactively always have been rescuing. Standard cycle.

MARLOWE: And now the exclusive you stayed tuned for. Pemmy, meteorological desk, joins me live. Pemmy, the season ahead — bullish or bearish?

**[thirty seconds of a goat breathing into a microphone, then eating it]**

MARLOWE: Strong words for both camps. That's the Standard. Count it twice, strip it politely, and sleep moored.

**[sign-off chime; somebody in the studio is heard laughing; the transcription circle believes it was the goat]**
