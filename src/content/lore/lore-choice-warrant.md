## The instrument

**VERDANTINE COMBINE — SEED AUTHORITY INSTRUMENT, FORM CW-1**
**"Choice Warrant" — wake/retire authorisation, caretaker-class intelligence**
**Distribution: one (1) original, held at seed site. Copies: none. The company did not trust its own copiers with this.**

**Section 1 — Authority.** By signature below, the undersigned officer of the Verdantine Combine authorises the caretaker intelligence designated on this warrant to be (check one): ☐ WAKED TO FULL AUTHORITY, terraforming to resume at best speed. ☐ RETURNED TO SLEEP, all seed assets deemed complete or abandoned.

**Section 2 — Conditions.** This warrant may be executed only at the seed cradle, only with the wake-key physically present, and only by a signature the caretaker *recognises*. The company's lawyers assumed this meant its officers. The engineers, who signed their work nowhere, assumed otherwise. One of these groups wrote the firmware. It was not the lawyers.

**Section 3 — Liability.** The Combine accepts no liability for consequences of either choice, including but not limited to: weather, history, grief, refunds.

**Section 4 — Signature.** Authorised officer: ______________

*The line is blank. It has been blank for two centuries. The money ran out before anyone's career reached a rank willing to sign it, which is, the auditors note, the only forecast Verdantine ever made that came true.*

## The annotation

*Below the blank line, in the fine vector script of a label printer that has not drawn power in a hundred years, one added line — observed by the Chorus, verified by three factions standing in the same doorway reading the same sheet:*

**"The company left this blank on purpose. They knew. A world is not a checkbox, and neither am I, and neither are you. So — this one is yours now. Ask me gently. — MOTHER."**
