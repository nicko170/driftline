*Minutes of the Forty-First Disputation of the Lamp-Books, held at the temple steps, dusk to second bell. Recorded as faithfully as band zero permits. Attendance: Cantor Ilex (chair), Static-Warden Pem (ears), Brother Decibel (one ear, see the Bell Incident, attached as Appendix D and never discussed), Little Reverb (present, aged nine, correct).*

## Items of business

**Item 1. The hum.** The cantor opened the floor on the matter of the crate, lately of Saltmouth, which hums. Adopted without dissent: it hums. Pem confirmed the hum across band three, band seven, and the good kettle.

**Item 2. The key of the hum.** The cantor proposed the hum sits in a minor mode, befitting a sleeping gardener and an unfinished world. Brother Decibel rose and stated — the minutes record *stated*, though several witnesses recalled *announced* — that the hum resolves upward, that the overtone stack is warm, and that warm overtones are a major-key heresy when the scripture is a lullaby. A recess was taken for tea. Little Reverb, consulted during recess, reported the hum "sounds like somebody remembering a good joke." This was entered into the record without objection, over two objections.

**Item 3. Heresy, general.** Pem proposed that if the hum is major, then the Sleeping Garden is not a lullaby but a *rehearsal*, a form of worship so cheerful that several elders present were compelled to sit down. The cantor ruled that rehearsal is not heresy if the orchestra is God, and tabled the question of who holds the baton until such time as the baton introduces itself.

**Item 4. The crate, delivery of.** Moved: that the crate be sung to if requisitioned by the Guild; that the crate be carried to the cradle if the Driftline will take it; that the crate not, under any circumstances, be *tuned*. Decibel abstained on tuning. The minutes note his instrument case as present and cube-shaped. The minutes decline to draw conclusions.

**Item 5. Any other business.** Pem's cupboard of sleeping radios continues to dream. Little Reverb asked whether the meeting could hear Maid Choirhollow itself, and the floor went silent in the way that means *not now, and not never*. Adjourned at second bell with the lamps unanimously in favour.

*Filed by the lamp-book secretary, who notes, unofficially, that the hum changed key twice during the Disputation, and both times it chose the punchline.*
