*Posted inside every job-board shelter on the line, hand-painted, faded, re-painted over the arguments. The commentary is not official. The commentary is the point. Commentary attributed where the writers owned up.*

---

## The creed

**ANY CRATE.**

Not any *legal* crate. Not any *comfortable* crate. If it's sealed, addressed, and paid for, it crosses. The Driftline does not open the mail. The Driftline has *opinions* about the mail, which it is entitled to keep in its helmet where they belong.

*(Ketch: "'Any crate' once meant forty kilos of live mud-wasps for a nutter in Choir Hollow who swore they were an orchestra. Delivered. Stung twice. Tenor section was excellent. This is the job. Stop asking if this is the job.")*

**ANY STORM.**

The storm does not care about your schedule, which is why you must. Shelter when the line says shelter; otherwise the only thing between two towns is you and a horizon cell, and the towns are both waiting.

*(Rill Davenant: "The creed doesn't say ride INTO the storm, it says any storm, which includes the option of laughing at one from inside a culvert with biscuits. Read closely, children. The subtext is biscuits.")*

**ANY DOOR.**

Every door. The Guild's counting house and the Choir's lamplight and the Reclaimers' shed with the toothy crusher. The Driftline kneels to nobody's flag, which is why everybody's flag pays us. Neutral doesn't mean nothing. Neutral means *everyone*, in order of postage.

*(Brinemaster Ogo, uninvited: "A courier who kneels is a wagon. We already have wagons. The wagons are cheaper.")*

## The codicils (unratified, universal)

1. If it's breathing, medevac outranks manifest.
2. You don't eat at a table you wouldn't deliver to.
3. The bike is yours the day the debt's paid. The *line* is yours the day you ride it.
4. If a courier goes quiet, the line goes looking. All of it. The creed doesn't say that. It does, though.

*(Tamsin Cho, in Guild ochre, which took courage: "Count it twice. Then trust the courier anyway. That's the whole economy, cowards.")*

---

*Footnote, added this season, ink still fresh, author unknown — though the shelter keeps its paint in Ketch's garage, and Ketch has the kind of handwriting that's mostly doors:*

*On the subject of ANY CRATE: there is a crate out there that hums when the storms come, on no manifest, paid for by nobody, addressed onward forever. The creed has seven words in it and every one of them now has a headache. We carry it anyway. That's what the words are for.*

*Last word to the founder, whoever she was, quoted third-hand and counting:* **"The desert decides what crosses. We decide it arrives."**
