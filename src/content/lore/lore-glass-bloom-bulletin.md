*Issued jointly and unwillingly by the Guild Bureau of Anomalies, observed by the Choir, audited by the Reclaimers, routed by the Driftline. Annotations are chronological, in four hands. Bulletin number 4 is the first to use the word "flowers."*

## The notice as issued

Riders are advised that crystalline growths — height one to nine metres, colouration teal-core with amber edge-light, structure *petalwise* — have appeared along the far shelf beyond the wind-farm ridge, in nineteen confirmed sites, all of them places old survey maps mark as dead conduit. The growths were not present last season. The growths were, per three sworn statements, **not present last week.** They hum when storms come. They are not cargo. Do not touch the blooms. Do not sell the blooms. Do not lick the blooms. *(This clause survives from Bulletin 2 and remains necessary.)*

## First hand — the Guild

*Anomalous asset emergence, unmanifested. Recommend shelving until ownership is established. Note: the blooms grow in geometric rows. Rows imply rows-*keeping*. Somebody is keeping rows out there, and I would like their books.*

## Second hand — the Choir

*Sisters, she gardens in her sleep. The litany said she would turn the desert over like soil when the key got close, and here is the ploughline, teal and singing. Do not shelve them. Do not sell them. Bring water to the door and stand where the petals point. They all point the same way. We checked at dawn. They all point at the cradle.*

## Third hand — the Reclaimers

*Bill of materials follows: not salvage. Cut one petal clean off a small bloom out past tower nine, worth a fortune in fused stock, and the stump wept molten glass and re-grew in two hours, *better* — new veins, brighter core, like it had been waiting for the pruning. We have stripped a hundred dead sites. This is not dead infrastructure. This is infrastructure that is **waking up and doing stretches.** We are keeping a respectful distance and a very long list of questions.*

## Fourth hand — the Driftline

*Route note for couriers: the blooms are not on any chart and the waypoints near them have started to drift — twenty, thirty metres, always *toward* the bloom-lines, like the desert tidying its own footpaths. Ride them as landmarks but fix your line on the ridge. And whatever the other three hands say — stop once. Kill the engine. The whole shelf hums in chord when the sun goes, and you will carry packages your whole life and nothing like it will ever be in one.*

*Final annotation, first hand again, much smaller: recommend immediate shelv—* the handwriting changes. *The ledger's descriptor field auto-completed today. It read: "garden, first attempts." We are no longer shelving this bulletin.*
