*Compiled by the Ledger Office, Keel Town branch, for the training of junior factors. Reading time: one sandstorm.*

## A form older than the Desert's patience

**Form 22-B, "Declaration of Cargo Which Does Not Appear on Any Manifest,"** is the oldest form in continuous Guild use and the only one that has grown longer under every guildmaster, including the one who campaigned on burning it. Its history is the history of the Desert refusing to be counted.

**Revision 1 (Year 12 of the Delay).** Four lines. *"Item found. Item described. Item's owner, if any. Signature."* It worked for nine years, until the Saltfish Mirage caravan declared "approximately bees" as cargo. The bees were not approximately anything; they were precisely and personally bees. Revision 2 added a field for *quantity, exact,* and a field for *quantity, honest.*

**Revision 4 (Year 41).** The ghost net. A fishing net — for a sea that had never existed — which washed up annually on the same salt flat, wet. Each year it was impounded; each year it left. Witness statements agreed on everything except basics. Revision 4 introduced the form's first philosophical field: *"Does the item appear to know something?"* Factors were instructed to leave it blank. Nobody has ever left it blank.

**Revisions 5 through 11** added pages: provenance, damnation status (ask the Choir), appraised value in salt-core AND by feel, a diagram for sketching the item "in repose" and "as it wishes to be seen." A notorious Revision 8 footnote — *"if the item is humming, hum back and note the interval"* — was removed in Revision 9 and restored in Revision 10 after two junior factors swore the humming *missed* them.

**Revision 12, current (Year 214).** Eleven pages. Its filing fee is one credit, waived in perpetuity for couriers, after the Driftline threatened to simply *carry everything without forms,* which the Ledger Office refers to as "the dark proposal."

## Appendix C (sealed)

**Appendix C, sealed, is the reason junior factors are trained on this form.** It records the filing of Form 22-B number 000001: submitted in the founding year, discovered *yesterday* in a cabinet that has been inventoried annually for two centuries. It describes, in accurate modern detail, a sealed courier crate, no sender, no weight, that hums when storms come. Signature of the filing factor: *illegible, or possibly "M."*

The Ledger Office's official position is a filing error across two hundred and fourteen years, which is an impressive error even for us. **Count it twice.** We did. That is the entire problem.
