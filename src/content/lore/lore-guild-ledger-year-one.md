*Held under manifest wax in the Saltmouth exchange, the Year One ledger of the company-store-that-became-the-Guild is the closest thing the desert has to a founding document. Excerpts are reproduced for couriers because the Guild believes everything crosses and everything's counted — including history.*

---

## Entries of record, Year One

**Entry 0001 — Waters, potable, drams held: 41,200. Souls, drinking: 3,811.** *Footnote, the first factor's hand: "Ten days to strictness, forty to thirst. The ships are not coming back down. We will now pretend this was always the plan."*

**Entry 0219 — Ration tickets, printed: 40,000. Ration tickets, forged: 0 (detected).** *"The forger was hired. Skill is skill."*

**Entry 0404 — Radios, working: 214. Wires, strung: none. Messages waiting: incorrigible.** *"M-Trask of the east tower proposed writing messages on slips and paying children to run them. The children organized by noon and unionized by dusk. It was beautiful. We bought them off with soup."*

**Entry 0412 — BOARD, POSTED, PUBLIC: one (1). Jobs chalked thereon: chimney swift nests (3), letter to Reva (1), grave to dig before the stone sets (1, weather permitting).** *"First entry on the first board. The Driftline does not exist yet. But look at it not existing."*

**Entry 0588 — Ice, cometary, remaining: substantial. Ice, cometary, price of: don't.** *"Phase Two shipped and the sender is gone. The desert's only inheritance fits a cold cellar. We do not charge inheritance tax on miracles; we charge storage."*

**Entry 0765 — Bikes, hover, functioning: 31. Riders, volunteer: 40. Riders, alive next season: see next year's ledger, column three, prepared in pencil.** *The Guild does not forgive this entry. The Guild prints it anyway. Some columns exist so that no one else has to learn them the hard way.*

**Entry 0899 — Salt, refined, sold to the crown of the ridge: twelve tonnes. Paid in: turbine blades, future considerations, and one (1) functional listening horn. Horn assessed: priceless, therefore worthless, therefore shelved.** *"The horn is in back room four. It hums. We count it twice."*

**Entry 1000 — Hope, misc., quantity: sufficient.** *"Audited annually. Never once found wanting. Reorder before the pity runs out."*

---

*Colophon, stamped in manifest wax: "Year One is the whole trick. Count what you have, price what you must, post the board, forgive the pencil. Everything after is arithmetic with better sunsets." — attributed to the first factor, name worn off the stamp, which the Guild insists is the correct ending.*
