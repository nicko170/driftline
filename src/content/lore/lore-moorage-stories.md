## Entry 441 — "The Docked Ghost."** Teller: Bric, mooring gang six.
So the *Kestrel's Debt* comes in on the night wind with no lanterns and no answer on the horn, and we catch her lines anyway, because a hull's a hull. Winch her in, nobody aboard. Galley's warm. Kettle's on. Logbook's open to a page that just says *owe you one*. Next morning the captain walks up from Keel Town with her whole crew — they'd come in early on a tender and slept ashore, and the Debt had slipped her moorings at the far dock, crossed thirty klicks of dark, and *come home by herself*. Ships do that, if they've been loved loud enough. We still owe the kettle one.
*Valuation: shift-and-a-half. Kettle debt referred to Article Two.*

## Entry 447 — "The Race With the Wind."** Teller: unnamed gull-rig pilot, refused to sign twice.
West rim, standing updraft, storm season knocking. My gull's light and the lift is strong and I decide — alone, to myself, which is the only race entry the wind accepts — that I'll beat the gust front to the mast. And I *do*, mast in reach, hand out — and the wind stops. Perfectly. Holds me there a full breath, close enough to read the dockmaster's expression, and then drops me on the ledger side like a lesson. Cost me a cargo bonus and a rib. Won the race. Lost the argument. The wind races everyone, friends. It just doesn't always tell you the stakes.
*Valuation: two shifts. Medical surcharge declined on grounds of narrative honesty.*

## Entry 463 — "The Mailbag That Sang."** Teller: Little Reverb, age nine, down from Lamp Rest with a courier escort.
The courier dropped the mail on the mast platform and the bag *hummed* — not broken-hum, engine-hum — *song*-hum, the one from the static litanies, Third Bell. Everybody heard it. The clerk heard it and his pen wrote it down before he decided to. Then it stopped and it was just mail, which is a kind of miracle too, but smaller. The courier said: *count it twice*. So we did. One bag, one song, zero explanation.
*Valuation: shift each for the whole gang, education supplement for the teller. Courier's escort fee paid in water and one (1) meal.*
