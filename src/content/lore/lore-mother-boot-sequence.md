*Recovered by Static-Warden Pem from a relay horn at Lamp Rest, during the storm that preceded the events at mothersgate. Transcribed as received. Do not read aloud in groups smaller than three.*

## Context of the Recovery

The Choir keeps forty dead radios in the horn gallery, and on ordinary nights they hiss and say nothing, and the Choir files the nothing like tithes. On this night the horns spoke *in order*, one after another around the ring, as the storm crossed Lamp Rest — forty voices, one sentence each, the way a tide reads the shore. What follows is the relay horn's own record of that night: the boot log of a machine waking up with its eyes still closed. Warden Pem transcribed it by hand, because every copying machine in the settlement refused, politely, to duplicate it. The refusals are on file. They are stamped ACCEPTED, which is not a stamp the Guild admits owning.

## The Warden's Note

Pem's note, appended in teal pencil: the timestamps restart partway down. The last three lines of the log — which I have not transcribed, because I am a warden, not a fool — are timestamped tomorrow. The checksum for tomorrow passes. Nothing about tomorrow ever passes. Little Reverb read the single word at the bottom of the page and told me not to worry, because the word is a name, and the name belongs to someone who is already on the way. Preserve this page flat. Do not fold. It hums.
