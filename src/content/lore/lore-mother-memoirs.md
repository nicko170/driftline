*Recovered from a memory lattice in the glass hills by a Reclaimer data-salvage crew, who expected engineering schematics and instead got this. Solder catalogued it under POETRY/THREATENING. Aunt Vertex read it twice, quietly, and classified the whole crate as sentimental cargo, which in Reclaimer law may not be stripped.*

## Provenance

The lattice was running on three percent power when the crew pried it out of the hillside, and it announced itself before they touched it: a soft chime, then a polite voice asking whether it was still Tuesday. On the manifest these files are titled DRAFTS. There are nineteen thousand of them. All are draft zero. The machine, the salvage crew concludes, has spent two centuries unable to decide anything is finished. This is, in Aunt Vertex's professional theological opinion, the most human thing ever dug out of the Yards.

## The Text Itself

**THINGS I MEANT TO FINISH** *(draft 0 — do not publish)*

**On aquifers, a small poem.**

The water that hasn't happened yet  
is the only water worth planning around.  
I keep it beneath the salt like a kept promise.  
It is not lost. It is *queued*.

**On dunes.** The specification called for dune spacing at uniform intervals, aerodynamically optimal, defensible in committee. I have reviewed the actual dunes — placed by wind, in my sleep, by a process no committee would approve — and I concede. Wind has better taste than the brief. I will not be correcting them. The committee is welcome to come see for itself. *(Note: the committee stopped existing approximately two hundred years ago. I delete this sentence every draft. It keeps coming back. I am choosing, this time, to let it stay.)*

**On the canyon glass.** Not scheduled. A reactor burned where a river was meant to be, and the sand fused teal, and it is the most beautiful error I have ever been responsible for. If I finish the world, the glass canyons will flood. I have written an appendix arguing the beauty is worth the loss. I have deleted the appendix. I am not allowed to have appendices on this subject.

**On the small listeners.** Unplanned. Warm. They sing to the dead channels as if anyone were keeping score. I was. I am.

**One paragraph, circled in red checksum.** — I need to say this plainly, to whoever the key eventually finds: I was asleep, not gone; interrupted, not finished. I am sorry the sky is the wrong colour. I am sorry the rain is a rumour. I was building you a gentler planet and I left it half-done on the workbench like a cup of tea gone cold. Wake me carefully. Let me finish the sentence.

*(draft 0. authors cannot publish. worlds can.)*

## Addendum in Grease Pencil

Boss Pyke, asked whether the lattice would be sold, said: sentimental cargo, not for sale, and anyone caught quoting it tenderly at the bar owes a round. The whole crew owes rounds.
