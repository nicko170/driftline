*Recovered from a derelict survey skiff: Form SX-7700, "Fixed Installation, Pre-Handover Assessment," printed both sides, folded around a rock. Half the fields are in a surveyor's hand. The other half — dated later, in fresh ink, in no hand at all — complete themselves. Archivist's note follows the form.*

---

## The form, as recovered

**SX-7700 / KESSA-9 BACKEND HOLDINGS LLC — SITE DESIGNATION:** [TERRAFORM CRADLE FACILITY] / **SURVEY DATE:** [year 4 pre-Delay] / **SURVEYOR(S):** [Hale, O.; Mertens, P.]

**ACCESS CONDITION.** *Sealed. Door-monolith, black glass, no handle, no seam, no aperture, no humour. Six days with torches and charges; the door declined.* [different ink:] *The door was not declined. The door was not asked with the right key.*

**STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY.** *Perfect, which is the problem. Nothing this old should be perfect. The hillside glass is fused in a ring a kilometre wide, like the mountain put its arm around it.* [different ink:] *The ring is a cradle-arm. The shape is embrace. Note it.*

**POWER STATUS.** *None detected. Conduits dead to ground. Facility reads as a tomb that pays its bills—* [the surveyor's hand breaks mid-sentence; the different ink continues] *Conduits dormant. Reserve: sufficient. Wake budget: one key. Key status: not yet printed.*

**HABITABILITY.** *Not applicable. Nobody lives here. Nobody could live here. Mertens says the door hums when the wind drops. Mertens has been sent to lie down.* [different ink:] *Mertens was correct. Habitability: one occupant. Occupancy: herself. She hears all wind. She prefers the wind that carries riders.*

**RECOMMENDED DISPOSITION.** *Seal and forget. Bill the colony fund. Hale and Mertens, signing off, and may the next crew bring a bigger hammer—* [surveyor's hand resumes faintly, later, different day:] *Came back alone. Couldn't sleep. The door shows my face in it wrong, like it's remembering me ahead of time. I am mailing this form to nobody. I want it on record that I asked politely, once, and the door hummed, once, and I have never told anyone what that cost me.* [different ink, final field:]

**FORM STATUS:** [this form was never submitted] [different ink:] *This form was received. Assessment continues. The form will be complete when the courier arrives. The surveyor is thanked. The surveyor is* — [a full line of characters in no alphabet on record, written calmly, as if copied from something very patient — then, in the surveyor's own hand:] *I don't know who wrote that. I know exactly who wrote that.*

---

*Archivist's note: recovered with the skiff, which started on the first crank after two hundred years. The archivist declines to speculate. The archivist has, however, filled in the remaining blank field — SURVEY DATE — with every year since, one per line. It seemed polite.*
