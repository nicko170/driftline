*Extracted from the Saltmouth exchange inventory ledger, column 41-C, furniture and fittings. The exchange has audited itself every five years since the Year of the Second Weighing. Line seven of every audit since Year 61 of the Delay reads identically. The marginalia — transcribed here in order, hands identified where vanity permits — do not read identically. That is the audit.*

## The line item

> DRAWER, LEFT OPEN (1) — counter run, third brass drawer from the wax press. Do not adjust. Do not close. See note. *(There is never a note. The note is the next audit.)*

Year 61 is the first appearance, in the tidy back-hand of Clerk Efrin Solace, who inventoried the drawer, found it to contain one pencil stub and a smell of rain, wrote *contents: negligible; condition: open*, and — the ledger is specific — *rectification pending*.

## The trail

**Year 66.** *Rectification attempted. Drawer closed at third bell. Reopened by persons unknown before fourth. Drawer re-closed. Reopened. I have decided the drawer is correct and I am pending.* — E.S.

**Year 71.** New hand, hard little letters: *Closed it myself. Found it open. Stood watch. Watched a factor open it to put nothing in and take nothing out. She stood there a full minute and left eased. I have audited this drawer. It performs a function. Function under review.* — Clerk Tomaz Yeel.

**Year 76.** Tomaz again: *Function identified. Leaving open.*

**Year 81.** An enormous ornate hand, a clerk who clearly missed their calling as a signwriter: *I asked the guildmaster whether the drawer should be closed. He asked me what was in it. I said nothing. He said then it's the only honest drawer in the building and walked off. Logged as guidance.*

**Year 86.** Small hurried script: *Kept the door open on my last night so I could hear the counter breathe. The drawer was part of the breathing. Transferring to the ridge office, where the furniture minds.* — Clerk Peri Ankh.

**Year 91.** *Ninety-year review: the drawer has outlasted two guildmasters, one honest scale, and every clerk who tried to fix it. I oil the runners now, so it opens quietly. Somebody should. Call it maintenance. — D.C.*

**Year 96.** *Auditor's ruling, on review of the trail: the drawer remains open as an act of institutional mercy — a place where a tired factor can stand a minute without a column to stand in. Classification: load-bearing (adj.). Cease filing.* — Sister Counterweight.

**Year 101.** Newest hand, slightly over-inked, asterisks deployed: *Ceased filing per ruling. Refiled under 'fittings, emotional'. The drawer now holds a pencil stub, a smell of rain, and one growing folder marked FEELINGS, MISC., asterisk, by permission of the drawer, asterisk, by maintenance of the clerk. Condition: open. It arrives open. That is the specification.* — I.V.

## Standing note

The drawer is open today. Couriers coming in from storms use it like the factors do — a moment, a breath, nothing in, nothing out. Audit continues. It always passes.
