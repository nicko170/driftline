*Foreword by Aunt Vertex, explosives: "Everything in this book fell on somebody before you got here. Things fall down; that is physics being honest. Your job is to be elsewhere when it does, and grateful after. The rest of these pages are manners."*

## Extraction rules

**Rule One — Read the building first.** A structure tells you its pulling order the way a carcass tells the knife. Seams, sags, scars from the burn days. Strip against the skeleton and the skeleton objects; strip *with* it and half your work falls politely into the sorting piles like it volunteered. The old plant in the Cinderflats has been dying gracefully for a hundred and fifty years. Grace is a blueprint. Learn to read it.

**Rule Two — Load-Bearing Historically.** Some members of a structure hold up nothing *physically* but hold up everything *legally, spiritually, or by widely-held local feeling.* Carved lintels, builder's plates, one (1) wall in the Yards known as Anchor Seven. Before cutting, ask: physical, historical, or haunted? The first is engineering, the second is Vertex's department, the third is Vertex's *other* department. Both departments charge consultation in butterscotch.

**Rule Three — The Pull.** Never alone (see Crusher, re: moods). Spotter on the flag line, cutter on the seam, one person doing nothing but *watching the sky,* because storms are also salvage and will happily start on you. Pull signal is the work song — you will know it by week two, everyone does — and when the song stops, *everything* stops, including conversations, blades, and grievances. The song stopping is the loudest sound in the Yards.

**Rule Four — Retiring a turbine, politely.** Windspine's old machines have stood their watch for two centuries. Protocol: drain the gearcase, tag the shaft, lower the blades *ourselves* — never let one spin down wild, it's undignified — and say the words. The words are: *"Turn's over. We've got it from here."* Then, and this is mandatory, a moment of quiet for whatever tired thing is buried in the gearbox humming back. Solder's got a list of the ones that hum back. The list is getting longer.

**Rule Five — If you wake something, you mind it.** Full doctrine in the sorting shed chalk. Summary: naming rights, feeding responsibilities, and a sincere apology to everyone within a hundred metres. *Signed, the Union. Appendix C is burnt. Appendix C was about the bees. Let it go.*
