*Transcribed at the garage cot, three nights running, by Ketch. Rill slept eleven hours at a stretch and counted through all of them. I wrote what I could keep up with. Where she drifted into song I've marked it. Where something else drifted in, I've marked that too, and I do not enjoy having marked it. — K.*

## Night One

Four-one-seven. Mark. Four-one-seven. *(repeats, ~40 min)* …keep the amber on the left shoulder… the door stands where the dunes agree… *(hums: the tune is "Flat Salt and a Following Wind", the old cradle lullaby; every note flat by the same small amount, like it's being retuned as it leaves her)* …four-one— *eight*. No. Seven. They moved the dunes. The dunes agreed to move. Funny. The dunes agreed.

## Night Two

*(no numbers for the first hour; instead she recited names — couriers, all of them, in Driftline order, including yours, Ash, she got to you near dawn and added:* "new one, good posture, the box chose well" *) …then, clear as a bell and not in her voice's rhythm: GRID FOUR-ONE-SEVEN IS APPROXIMATE. CORRECTION: THE DOOR STANDS AT FOUR-ONE-SIX POINT NINE. *(I wrote it down exactly. I checked the map. The corrected grid is past the far glass hills, at the sealed survey site the old charts call the cradle. I have decided to stop deciding things after midnight.)*

## Night Three, Near Sunrise

…don't let the season find a gap between you and her… *(she was remembering the shelter line, saying your orders back, Ash — she heard every word you said on that wagon and filed it under awake-enough-to-keep)* …the crate is a key, the cave is a lock's ear… the Choir is right about the wrong things, the Guild counts the right things wrong, the Reclaimers know how doors open and not what doors are *for*… tell Ketch the four hundred credits are *spent*, spent on — *(she laughed in her sleep, first normal sound in three days)* — on *momentum*.

*End of transcription. Rill woke for good on the fourth morning, asked for breakfast and whether anyone had fed her goggles. She does not remember the correction. The radio in the corner, which has been dead since before Ash was born, was warm. — K.*
