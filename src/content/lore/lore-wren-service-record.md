*Bound in oilskin, kept in the seat pannier where the manual used to be before the manual was traded for soup. Transferred with the bike, per Driftline custom: the log belongs to the machine, the machine only lends itself to riders.*

---

## Owner 1 — Pell Anyanwu, windspine survey tech, seasons 1–39.**
Entries in a neat engineer's hand, mostly torque values. Season 4: *"Ridge gust took the vane housing. Rebuilt from survey scrap. She flies truer angry."* Season 12: *"Dent, port fin. Cause: goat (confirmed, witnessed, unrepentant)."* Season 31: *"Left gyro cage developing Opinions. Rebalanced. Opinions persist. Have decided they are character."* Last entry: *"Promoted to desk. Selling her cheap to someone who'll actually ride. If that's you: she likes the morning wind off the flats. So did I."*

## Owner 2 — Mele 'Half-Lap' Sorren, courier, Glassroad route, seasons 40–61.**
Entries shorter, louder, occasionally scorched. *"Boost cell replaced AGAIN. The glass does not forgive but it does slide."* *"Dent, nose fairing. Cause: winning."* *"Dent, port fin. Cause: losing."* *"Race with Jett's uncle — do not ask Ketch about this entry."* Season 61: *"Arm's done. Doctor says desk, wife says desk, arm agrees with both. Bike goes to Ketch at Saltmouth because he's the only person rude enough to keep her honest."*

## Saltmouth garage — holding entries, Ketch's hand, seasons 61–67.**
*"In. Full strip. Half of her is survey scrap and the other half is optimism."* *"Horizon cell original. Still hums like a man thinking about lunch. Some things Verdantine built right, presumably by accident."* Season 67: *"Sold to a new courier. Guild financed; the kid owes eight thousand on her, which is robbery, and I won't tell the kid that either. Underpriced her. Sue me."* And below that, smaller, in pencil, as if hoping the page might forget it:

*"Good bike. Don't tell her."*

## Owner 3 — A. Varga, courier, Driftline.**
First entry: *"Season 68. She hums higher than the manual says is calm. The manual was soup. We're learning each other."*
