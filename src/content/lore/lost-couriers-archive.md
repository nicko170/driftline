*The back ledger of the Saltmouth garage, rebound three times. Ketch keeps it. Nobody asked Ketch to keep it; a keeper keeps the door, and this is the other door. Entries are one line each, by rule — the garage says a courier's life deserves more than a page and less than a sermon. Format fixed since the first entry: name, bike, last route, last radio word.*

---

## The entries (selection, by era)

**Mara Dec, "the first, probably."** Bike: unknown, argued about forever. Route: the flats, before the flats had names. Last word: *"further."* The archive's oldest line and its whole thesis. The creed's commentary here is one sentence: she was not lost; she went first, and the archive is where we keep the difference.

**Toll Anderssen.** Bike: *Vocation*. Route: Driftline 2, storm season, carrying mail through a Grazer the Guild rated outrunnable. Last word: *"visible."* He was reporting visibility. The report holds. His line is read aloud every season-opener, because the Guild's ratings have been wrong twice since and right only in the way a stopped clock is right.

**Efi Marrow.** Bike: *Satchel*. Route: night supply window, Keel Town arm. Last word: *"lamp."* Beacon 14's lamp failed that night. The tender was found asleep at the wick; nobody blamed him aloud, and the tenders have kept double-watch since, unpaid, which is the archive working as intended. There is always one more person awake since Efi.

**Corin Ode.** Bike: *Grammar*. Route: Choir Hollow supply, four-day crossing. Last word: *"answered."* Nobody knows what answered him. The Choir asked to read his entry; Ketch brought the ledger to the Hollow personally, held it while they read, and rode it home the same night. The Choir has never asked again. The line stays in.

**Sister Hale Ode.** Bike: *Margin*. Route: the same crossing, eleven years later, unasked, retracing. Last word: *"found."* Her entry sits beside her father's, in his daughter's hand first, then Ketch's. The archive allows additions by whoever the missing were carrying. This one weighed the most by some measure the ledger doesn't have a column for.

**Sen Pritchard.** Bike: *Dowry*. Route: canyon post, first wall of the season. Last word: *"updraft."* Sen rode the wall's lift nine hundred metres and the radio carried her laughing. The entry is the archive's most-visited. Couriers touch it before storm runs the way you'd touch a doorframe leaving home.

**Rill Davenant.** Bike: *Wren* — the archive notes the bike in service, second-hand, currently. Route: withheld at the keeper's discretion, per the one rule Ketch wrote into the ledger personally: *an entry in progress is not an entry.* Last word: withheld. The page is in. The line is not crossed. Couriers reading the archive pass that page like passing a sleeper.

*At the back, unnumbered, in Ketch's hand, the archive's only editorial: "The board says delivery is the creed. The board is young. The creed is: come home. This ledger is the creed's memory, and its memory is one line long per person, and the line is never 'undelivered.' The archive closes each entry filed. It has, to date, never been full."*
