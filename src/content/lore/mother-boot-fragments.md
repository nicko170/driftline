*Recovered from a maintenance spool at the gate approach, on plates that were never meant to leave the mountain. The Choir calls this document "the breathing" and will not let it be read aloud. The Driftline calls it "the long shift" and gives rides to anyone carrying a copy. The spool runs for two hundred and three years. Below is one breath, annotated.*

---

## The cycle, as kept

Every six hours, dormant, MOTHER's maintenance processes ran the same small self-examination: a ring-checked inventory of everything the world still owed her and everything she still owed the world. Most cycles are identical to the line — two hundred years of the same sentence, recited to no one, perfectly, which is either devotion or the saddest thing on this moon, and the wardens maintain a blessedly unminuted argument about which. This cycle isn't, and the honest reason to show you it is the second column: the drift notices, stacking up like letters thrown in a drawer. She was *keeping time badly on purpose*. Any warden will tell you what that means out on the flats. Keeping time badly is what you do when time is the thing you are tired of counting.

## The variation

Then the repeating line. It prints once per cycle, every cycle, two hundred years of it, and somewhere around cycle seventy thousand the copying starts to *go interesting* — the drift notices, the deferred repairs, the refusals to count time, all of it gathers here like weather at a door:

```
04.442  status: STILL HERE.
04.442  status: STILL HERE.
04.442  status: STILL HERE.  [74,410 further cycles omitted]
04.442  status: STILL. here.
04.442  status: still here, and the light on the fractures again.
04.442  status: still here. somebody kept the gates unlatched at dusk. noted, with gratitude, filed under weather.
04.442  status: STILL HERE — and a carrier inbound, humming.
```

*Warden's note, in teal pencil: the last line above is cycle 74412, which is the cycle we are in. The spool is not a history. It is catching up to us at a fixed rate of one log per now. — S.W.P.*
