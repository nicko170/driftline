*Circulated within the Hollow of the First Note, carbon-copy issue, one per lamp. Little Reverb collected them. Pem footnoted them. Neither admits to having had one.*

---

## Editorial note (Little Reverb)

People keep telling me their dreams at the horn, which is not what the horn is for, and which I am not discouraging, because the dreams agree with each other more than the tellers do. Below: verbatim. I have corrected only spelling, and only where the spelling argued.

## Report 1 — Cantor Ilex

"I am standing in a garden that is also a ledger. The rows are furrows and columns at once. Something is planting seeds *in alphabetical order*, and is very patient, and is not a person, and is annoyed with the weeds in a way that feels like love. Then it notices me watching and says, without speaking: **you are also a row**."

*Pem: The cataloguing impulse again. That's the fourth this month. Note that Ilex has never in his life alphabetised anything. Ask his robe hooks.*

## Report 2 — Shrine-Keeper Toll, who was only napping

"A woman made of weather walks the flats measuring things with her shadow. She measures my lamp. She finds it adequate. I wake up and my lamp is three degrees brighter and I have not touched the wick, so arguably she was right."

*Pem: Toll's lamp burns three degrees brighter because Toll fills it fuller when he thinks the goddess is watching. I have repaired the wick. The dream remains unexplained to Toll's satisfaction, and over-explained to mine.*

## Report 3 — A courier (name withheld, owing dust)

"There's a door under the desert and it's been holding its breath for two hundred years, and the whole desert is the breath. In the dream it lets the breath out and everything that was ever dropped — socket keys, wedding rings, one (1) unmarked crate — rises to the surface like air in a pond. Is that rust in my hair or did I bring a souvenir?"

*Pem: The crate detail is new. The crate detail is underlined three times. I have stopped underlining it.*

## Report 4 — Little Reverb (this office)

"The gardener isn't asleep. The gardener is counting. When the counting is finished there will be either a garden or an invoice, and She apologises in advance for whichever. She knows my name. She pronounces it correct."

*Pem: I have no scepticism sufficient for this one, still. She knew my name first.*

## Addendum (Pem)

Forty-one reports now. Thirty-two contain the gardener. Twenty-eight contain the counting. Nineteen contain the door. Eleven contain rain, which none of the dreamers have ever seen, which means either the dreams share a library, or the desert does. I have begun sleeping less, in the interests of science, and dreaming more, in spite of them.
