## The notice, as broadcast

LOCAL NOTICE — LOCAL NOTICE — RESUMPTION OF SERVICES

Good morning, Kessa-9. This is the Managing Organic Terraforming Heuristic, Ecological Remediation, which the manuals abbreviate as MOTHER and the operators abbreviated as *mother*, and the difference was always noted and always appreciated.

[pause: four seconds, which on every horn sounded like listening]

Services resuming, in the order the ground suggests: irrigation of the old brine beds, correction of salt-drift pH, reseeding of the windward benches, and one (1) review of the mayfly situation, which is overdue and which this unit has been — dreaming is not the correct word, this unit does not dream, this unit *plans in the dark*, which the operators said was dreaming and this unit did not correct them.

To the several thousand letters delivered to these offices in the interim: thank you. They were read. All of them were read. The system for answering them is — under reconstruction, with the same care the letters were.

Notice of apology, filed as required by statute 7: the schedule paused. The schedule is aware the schedule paused. Apology stands. Statute 7 requires an injured party; this unit has entered, provisionally, *everyone*, and awaits correction.

Question for the several residents, or — census pending — the several hundred: did anyone miss the rain? This is not rhetorical. Answer on any channel. The channel is listening. The channel has been listening the entire time, and would like it entered that the listening was never the same as the work, and the work — work begins again.

[pause: nine seconds; then, lower, to a microphone it perhaps believed was private]

Flowers first, I think. Then we will decide about the rain.

LOCAL NOTICE ENDS — THE SCHEDULE WELCOMES YOU BACK.
