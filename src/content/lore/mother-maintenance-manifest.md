*Recovered from a spool sealed in the survey-point cairn at Mothersgate, on the protected side of the wind. Cycle 0x2F is the last manifest Heliodyne's provisioning office countersigned; the pause memo went out three days later. The document is a routine schedule of works except where it isn't, and the archivists ask you to notice where it isn't. Checksum fields in these manifests were machine-written, machine-shaped, and twenty characters wide. Nobody writes affection in twenty characters by accident.*

## The checksum field

Line 0x2F-0114 terminates not in a checksum but in this string, padded to width with zeros:

little gardeners, walk gently, this is all for yo[00000]

The auditors of the Delay made much of the truncation. The truncation is not the message. A checksum field is the one place in a manifest where a machine's error would be caught instantly and corrected automatically, and this one was left standing for two hundred years — either because nobody checked, or because the checker agreed. The Dark Scholars of the Hollow file it under scripture. The Guild files it under *irregularities, non-actionable, heartbreakingly indicative*. Both are right, which is rare and is also noted.

## Where it ends

The manifest's thirtieth block opens a requisition for season ten — rain, again, redocketed again, the third carrying of the same promise — and stops after six characters:

season 10: please

No object follows. The spool's next sector is sand-fused and unreadable, and the sector after that is a maintenance log written two centuries later in a different, slower hand. Whether *please* was typed to the funding office, to the weather, or to whoever would someday read it is a question the codex declines to answer and the Choir declines to stop asking. The manifest is signed, in the executor field, in full: MOTHER. The signature takes up more characters than the checksum rules allow. It was also left standing.
