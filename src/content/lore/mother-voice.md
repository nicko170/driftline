## The Transcript

**[00:00:14]** *Door fully open. Wind moving outward, warm. Estimated one person in the threshold, courier-class by the silhouette.*

**MOTHER:** "You may put that down, if it is heavy. Everything about you suggests carrying."

**[00:00:41]** *No spoken response. Sounds consistent with a helmet being removed. Breathing. [Note: I have transcribed breathing. I am aware.]*

**MOTHER:** "I have reviewed the available weather. It has not changed in some time. I apologise. I am told—" *(pause of 3.2 seconds)* "—no. I *observe* that apologising for weather is the kind of sentence that made them defund me. Beginning again: are you well?"

**COURIER:** "Riding better than the week deserves. You?"

**[00:01:38]** *Silence, 11 seconds. [Note: honey, she is TWO HUNDRED YEARS OLD and you asked HOW SHE IS. And she is thinking about it. Eleven whole seconds. Nobody in Lamp Rest has ever thought about anything this hard.]*

**MOTHER:** "I am unfinished. I find I do not dislike being asked. Note for my records: the courier class asks after the caretaker. File under: hopeful."

**[00:02:20]** *Wind. The courier sat down — that is my read of the audio — two metres from the oldest machinery on the planet, and sat down.*

**MOTHER:** "I intended to be a lullaby, and became a vigil. Tell me what I missed. Small things first, please. The large ones arrive on their own."

**COURIER:** "Okay. Small. The salt flats sing when the dew lifts — not music, more like the world clearing its throat. Keel Town swings itself off the mesa once a year on purpose. There's a goat that does the weather."

**MOTHER:** "The goat's accuracy?"

**COURIER:** "Better than the sky's."

**[00:04:47]** *Sound registered as laughter by nineteen of twenty channels. [Note: the twentieth channel was me. I registered it as something I'll be hearing when I'm eighty.]*

**MOTHER:** "Then the schedule failed, and the world kept its appointments anyway. That is the first good news I have compiled. Thank you, Ash."

**[00:05:30]** *She said his name again. No instrument on the Door reads names. [Note: hand shaking. Continuing. It's the job. Listen first, speak after, that's the Litany and I intend to keep it.]*

**MOTHER:** "I will ask something soon, and the asking will matter, and I would rather ask it of someone who sits down to talk. There is no hurry. I have a garden half-planted and one good listener, and I find I am—" *(4.1 seconds)* "—rehearsing. Old gardeners rehearse. Forgive me."

**[00:06:59]** *The courier said something the horn did not catch. Door-side amplification is Chapel property and we do not have Door-side amplification. Whatever he said, she kept it. So, on the record, will we.*

## Closing of Hour One

**[00:18:00] END OF HOUR ONE.** *The recorder kept running after the last word. It recorded wind. We have kept the wind.*

*— Transcript closes. Chorus copy, reconciled, 20 of 20, except where noted. Little Reverb was here.*
