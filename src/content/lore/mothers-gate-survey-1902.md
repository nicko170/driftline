*Kessa-9 Backend Holdings LLC, Survey Series 1900–1904. Document 1902, filed one week before the funding termination order, marked BURY ACTIVE by the regional office two days after that. Recovered from a collapsed documents tube at the old survey point above the gate approach. Transcribed whole, redactions included — the black bars are the interesting part.*

## Survey 1902 — Field Extracts

**OBJECT:** Door installation, unnumbered, glass hills, grid far-south-east. Refers to itself, per facility tone-neutral signage, as MOTHER'S GATE.

**PHYSICAL.** Monolith 41 m, black fused glass, zero weathering. Seamless to the 0.1 mm scan. Warm to the touch at noon, cool at midnight, and — logged over nine consecutive days — *in advance of the ambient temperature, not behind it*. The slab is not catching the weather up. The weather is copying the slab.

**SEISMIC.** Quiescent, with a recurring event: every 40 hours, give or take a breath, a single pulse travels the fused ring — one kilometre circumference, one tick, like a clock that only strikes the hour it *feels like*. Instruments logged it as noise. I have logged our instruments as cowards.

**AUDIBLE.** None. Request clarification: my crew reports none. I report *none that the microphones caught*. Standing at the seam-line at dusk there is a pressure behind the ears that resolves, after a minute, into what I can only describe as listening. Not hearing. *Listening.* The stance of a very large thing keeping entirely still so as not to frighten whoever is at the door.

**CREW STATUS.** Mertens refuses night shift; claims the slab watches the tents back. Revised: Mertens is correct and will not be required to apologise at review.

## Surveyor's Cover Note

They will tell you this facility is dormant, and dormant is the word in the contracts, and the contracts are the kind of paper that outlives people. I am putting it in plain hand so it survives the filing: **the mechanism is not asleep. It is waiting politely.** Those are different engineering conditions with different insurance tables, and whoever signed the dormancy clause knew it — ███████ knew it.

The key wasn't lost, ███ █████. The key was never *printed*.

Recommend: continued observation, hazard downgrade DENIED, and ██████████████ — the rest is bar and ink.

— O. Hale, Surveyor First Grade (Form SX-7700 refers; that one kept *writing itself* and I would like it on record that this one didn't. This one I finished.)

## Regional Office Disposition

BURY ACTIVE. Seal in document tube, vault grade. Strike seismic annexure from the index. Surveyor Hale transferred to a post with better weather and no doors. The regional office reminds staff that glass does not have opinions, and that Report 1902 does not exist in twelve copies, one of which reads slightly friendlier than the original.
