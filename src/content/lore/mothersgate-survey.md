## SURVEY SUMMARY — CRADLE APPROACH, FINAL (pre-seal)

**SURVEYOR OF RECORD:** Chief Field Surveyor I. Okafor-Bligh, TCI (Terraform Cradle Installation). **PURPOSE:** certify the approach corridor for handover and close the file.

**ELEVATIONS, APPROACH RING, CLOCKWISE FROM TRUE NORTH (metres over salt datum):** 301, 299, 302, 300, 301, 299, 302, 300. Field note: a kilometre-wide ring of fused hillside, embossed like something cupped in a palm, and the four values repeat *exactly*. Glass does not anneal into repeating integers. I re-shot three stations at dawn and got the same numbers, which is when I stopped re-shooting stations.

**ANOMALOUS READINGS.** Ground-penetrating return shows the corridor is not a corridor. It is a *throat*: the decking, the piers, the fused ring — all of it reads as one manufactured shape aimed inward at the door, and there is no scan depth at which the shape ends. Subsurface tone anomaly: the rock carries a standing resonance of extremely low frequency, present only when unobserved — every instrument we pointed at it reported silence and a calibration fault. One (1) instrument was left "broken" overnight, aimed, with its alarm disabled. It recorded eleven hours of a slow, even pulse. I have not included the tape. I have included this sentence so that someone knows there was a tape.

**STRUCTURAL.** The door (see project drawings: "monolith-type closure, black glass, handleless") is in perfect condition after a furnace-wind that scrubs this flank of the hills down to bone every season. Nothing here weathers. Weathering is the only language these hills speak; whatever refuses it is speaking something else.

**PERSONNEL NOTES.** Two of my crew asked, separately, on different days, whether the door "knows we're leaving." Both were decent, dull people until this posting. I am recording the question rather than the answer because the answer was a long silence, and silence in a survey is data.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Seal it, bill it, forget it.

**ACTION TAKEN (copyist's note, years on):** sealed, per drawing; billed, per invoice HX-2, disputed for a decade and paid by nobody; forgotten — *this field was never completed. The file is open. The corridor is intact. The ring's elevations still repeat. Recent relay-band chatter attributes a hum to the site that matches the resonance this survey declined to report. The surveyor's marginal note, last page, in pencil, addressed to no one: "We didn't seal it to keep something in. I had not finished being wrong about which."*
