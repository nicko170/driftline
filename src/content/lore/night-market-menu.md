*Broadcast from the market crane winch-house on still nights only — a moving town cannot hold a menu steady and a swaying one cannot hold a listener. Prices are binding until first sway. Read by Hespa Vole, winch-keeper, who claims she is not paid for this and does it for the acoustics. — transcription circle*

## Appetites of the hanging town

Good evening, dangles and danglers. Market is OPEN and the town is true. Weigh yourself before you eat — the charter's counting, not me.

**Stall one, the Crane End: salt-baked lichen loaves,** wind-dated — this batch rode a west wind so it tastes of old snow and somebody's chimney, four salt-beads the loaf, six if you argue about the snow. **Storm bread, pre-raised,** crust-patterned in threes — *yes*, FENSK, it is authentic static, you watched it scorch, I saw you watching, twelve beads and you eat facing west like a person of consequence.

**The middle decks: the noodle matter.** Two stalls, one feud, no settlement, as is tradition. **Pell's Deluxe Hand-Pulled** — thin style, broth built on drippings and *confidence* — I am NOT reading what's on the card, Pell, my contract says prices and so does the envy clause — fine. *Pell's:* thin noodles, ochre broth, seven beads, neon sign that says NOODLES in letters you can see from the flats, which is the entire problem according to **Maud's Kettle & Vigil,** thick-cut noodles, broth the colour of a held grudge, six beads, and Maud has asked me to announce — I quote — "a real noodle carries the soup; a Pell noodle is ADVERTISING." Pell has counter-announced, in writing, that Maud's broth has been described by travellers as "structural." Six and seven beads, respectively, and I am charging *twelve* to anyone who asks me to pick a side, because picking a side is view-credit for the soul.

## Provisions, priced as of bell

Dried glassbloom hearts — legal this season, legal ONLY this season — nine beads. Spice caddy, four-pinch: rustpepper, lighthouse salt, harsh amber, and a pinch of whatever Wisp brought back from the far moorage— fifteen beads, *no questions is the price.* Water: still free. It says so in chalk. It will always say so in chalk.

## Weather

Forecast courtesy of the Guild desk at Saltmouth, which is a goat. Pemmy stood still on the basin map today. Readings indicate: fine tonight, fine tomorrow, and — the goat lay down at sunset and we are honouring that locally regardless of distance. If the goat says no, the goat means *all of us.* Final orders, final bell, and Hespa Vole is climbing down.

Last item, not on the menu, pricing requests refused: a courier came through at dusk asking after a humming box. I said we deal in food. The *stairs* said otherwise, the way they creaked. That's free of charge. Sleep moored, hang true.
