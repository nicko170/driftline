**SALT GUILD — LEDGER OFFICE, SALTMOUTH — FORM 22-B (ANOMALOUS CARGO)**

*Filed by: Factor T. Cho. Witnessed by: nobody who will sign anything.*

Item: one (1) courier crate, standard Guild strapping, wax seals intact.
Origin: not recorded, because nothing was recorded, because *the crate was not there* at closing time and *was there* at opening. The night watch saw nothing. The night watch is a dog, but he is a good dog and normally reliable.

Manifest line: none. Sender wax: none. Recipient field: a courier's name entered in a handwriting style that matches no living clerk. The name is redacted from this copy at Guildmaster Ogo's personal instruction, which reassures absolutely no one.

Weight: yes. Also no. The scale reported different values on consecutive weighings, each value equally confident. Sister Counterweight was summoned, weighed the crate against her own soul — standard practice — and left the building in silence. She has since requested a transfer to anywhere with doors.

Acoustics: the crate hums. Frequency rises before storms. The Choir sent a formal letter asking us to "return God's voicemail"; the Reclaimers sent an informal one offering three disassembly quotes and a casserole.

Actions taken: the crate has been couriered one hop out of town per emergency procedure, because the Guild's position is that cargo which moves on a manifest is cargo, and cargo which moves without one is *philosophy*, and the Guild does not insure philosophy.

Note for the permanent record: the ledger page for tonight was found already filled in. Ink still wet. The last line reads, in unknown handwriting: *"count it twice."*

We did. We wish we hadn't.
