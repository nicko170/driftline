*Found folded into a coil of grip tape, Keel Town, paid for in full. Madame Traction denies writing the last entry. She does not deny it loudly.*

## Catalogue of the Drawer (partial, do not request the rest)

**1 socket key, pre-Delay, honest.** Fits the old moorage winches. Price: 40 cr and the story of where you found it, *in full, dear*. Items change hands. Stories stay.

**1 horn mouthpiece, Choir issue, decommissioned.** Sold back to me by the decommissioned party under cover of lamp-light. Price: what you paid for silence, plus freight. I will know if you sing into it. Everyone will know if you sing into it.

**221 weather predictions, caprine, certified.** Provenance impeccable; accuracy agricultural. Price: 6 cr the dozen, or free with the purchase of a sense of humour. No refunds for rain.

**1 skyship bell, small, name filed off.** The filing is fresh, which is the interesting part, which is why it is in the Drawer and not on the shelf. Price: not today. Ask again when the filing is old and the wound is older.

**3 horizon cells, charged by someone else's storm.** Warranty void by definition. Price: 90 cr each, and I will not ask your name, which is itself part of the price. Put it in the ledger under *gratitude*.

**1 courier's badge, Driftline, worn smooth.** The oath says hold the line. The oath does not say what to do with the badge when the courier doesn't. Price: free to any courier who can tell me the line they're holding, and mean it. Nobody has yet claimed it. The badge is patient.

**1 manifest, blank, sealed, Guild wax unbroken.** Do not open it. It is not mine, strictly, and it is not yours, period. Price: —

*Below, in a hand that slopes the wrong way and uses an ink nobody pours any more:*

**1 reproduction of the future, printed on demand, humming.** Price: one (1) debt, called in when the door opens. Terms held in confidence by the party of the first part, who is not the shopkeeper and is not, yet, you. Not for sale. **Already sold. Already shipped. Ask the new girl with the second-hand bike.**

*The card stops there. The drawer didn't, apparently. Madame Traction has since moved it and declines to say where, except that "some drawers should be walked past quickly, dear, especially the ones you've already delivered."*
