*Aired on* Low Sun, High Water*, the zine-wire out of Saltmouth, as an audio feature. The interview was conducted at speed through the Drowned Array's panel-rows, per the subject's conditions, which were given in chalk. Host: Denna Voss. Subject: [unverifiable]. Transcriber's notes in brackets. Where the recording fails, the chalk provided clarification.*

## Part One: Names and Markers

**VOSS:** State your name for the record.

**KID:** [two short engine revs, one long]

**VOSS:** The record does not accept revs.

**KID:** [long pause] Significant.

**VOSS:** ...The record accepts that, apparently. Why do you shave course markers off the training loops? It's vandalism, isn't it?

**KID:** *Inventory.* [the chalk, on a passing panel: THEY GROW BACK SLOWER THAN I TAKE THEM. ECOLOGICAL.]

**VOSS:** Moving on. What do you eat? Riders report seeing nothing but warm soda crates at your cairn drops.

**KID:** *Efficient.* [a second chalk mark: the glyph for soup, then a cross through it, then the glyph for soup again, thoughtfully]

**VOSS:** There are parcels leaving Saltmouth marked CARETAKER, ARRAY. The Driftline won't discuss them. Will you?

**KID:** [silence] [visibly, the subject banks through a gap journalists describe as structurally enthusiastic] *Stocked. Restock. Repeat.* Caretaker.

**VOSS:** And the rider community — do you consider yourself one of them? You race them. You never race *with* them.

**KID:** Later.

**VOSS:** Later you'll join, or later you'll answer?

**KID:** [one word, flat as a weather report] *Yes.*

## Part Two: The Paragraph

**VOSS:** Fine. Professional question, then. You've shaved seconds off every marked route on the flats. What's the trick? In your own— in however many words.

**KID:** [the Subject slows; the visor-grin tips at the sun; the engine drops to a hum that our engineer called 'deliberate,' and then the Subject speaks the only full paragraph on record, in a voice younger than the legend, patient and very exact] A drift isn't losing the line. It's lending the corner your straight. You arrive wide, you pay the bend its angle early, with interest, and the corner *gives you back the speed* on exit as change. Everybody thinks the glass is slippery. Glass is *generous*. Nobody asks it properly. [a beat] Charged the soda.

**VOSS:** ...One more question. Who are you?

**KID:** [two short revs, one long, already a hundred metres gone, and on a panel behind the finish line, in fresh chalk:] GOOD QUESTION. ASK THE ARRAY.

*[The Low Sun editorial team wishes to state that the Subject returned the microphone. Postage paid. Warm soda enclosed.]*
