## FORM 1-R — RISK ASSUMED, GRINS NOTWITHSTANDING

The undersigned ("the Optimist") acknowledges that canyon racing on fused glass involves, without limitation: speed, glass, other Optimists, wind, pride, and consequences (hereafter "the Syllabus").

**Clause One.** The Optimist waives all claims against the event, the road, the sky, and the concept of friction. *Annotation, knife-cut, old: "friction filed a counterclaim and lost."*

**Clause Two.** Finishing first constitutes victory. Finishing constitutes honesty. Starting constitutes the tailwind we all ride, friend.

**Clause Three.** No Optimist shall cut a gate, shorten a gate, move a gate, or *become* a gate. *This clause exists because of the Gate Ethics Precedent, in which a racer — name redacted, initials J.M. — argued in open hearing that the final gate was "more a guideline than a geometry," won by half a bike-length, and was awarded the line and stripped of the time, a verdict nobody understood and everybody respects.*

**Clause Four.** The defending champion is entitled to talk. The champion is not entitled to be believed. All claims made by the champion carry an implied asterisk\*. *\*The asterisk is load-bearing. Do not remove the asterisk. — apparently this clause was written by the champion, about the champion, for the champion's own protection, which is the closest thing to humility on record at this gate.*

**Clause Five.** In the event of a tie, both Optimists are declared wrong and must ride it back.

**Clause Six.** The road is older than the race. The glass was once a mistake of unprecedented size. Race it kindly.

## Signatures

The below signed in **three inks**: ochre for the Guild spotter (who takes the bets), teal for the Choir witness (who prays for the field, impartially, allegedly), rust for whoever's currently losing money.

*Fifty-one names. One name appears as both Optimist and witness, which is Clause-nullifying and was allowed to stand because the alternative was telling her.*
