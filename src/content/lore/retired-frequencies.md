*From Book 41 of the Register of the Patient Dead, kept at the cache cairn of the Drowned Array. The register is kept in two hands: the unit hand (block capitals, exact, unembarrassed) and the warden hand (ink, leaning, inclined to mercy). Entry format per the old Arrangement: call-sign, band, years of service, final transmission, cairn number, condition on receipt.*

## Selected entries

**KSM-1 — "Harbor Voice."** Band one, public weather, sixty-one years. Final transmission: tide tables for a sea that had been gone eleven years already. Received rusted, correct, unashamed. Cairn 9. — *Warden hand: it read tides to the sand and the sand attended. The desert keeps what we give it. It is rude to give it nothing.*

**KGL-7 — "the Road's Throat."** Relay mast, Glassroad south span, forty years. Final transmission: a toll notice, half-sung, logged by the receiving clerk as "probably fine." Received still singing, faintly. Cairn 14. Chalk pre-correct. — *Warden hand: it sings when the wind crosses east. Passing toll-takers are instructed to harmonise once and ride on. It counts as a fee.*

**KPR-2 — "the Claiming Bell."** Private charter band, nine years. Final transmission: wedding vows, relayed clean through a sand wall that killed every other channel on the dial. Received dented. Cairn 31. — *Unit hand: the bell out-broadcast the storm by reason of better motivation. This unit has modelled the incident. This unit no longer models the incident.*

**Row 40.** *[Blank in both hands.]* — *Reserved per the Warden. Some years a frequency retires with nothing to write down. The blank is the record. Do not dust it.*

## The newest entry

**KAR-9 — "Mast Nine,"** listening horn, firstborn of the crater. Two hundred and three years of service. Final true transmission: a carrier wave received from nothing, answered at eight kilohertz by something below. Thereafter, repeats only — the station's own weather reports returned, in the Warden's phrasing, "wearing better diction." Voice-box received at burial on the 26th of Harvest, still warm, escorted the whole road by its warden on foot and one (1) courier, at funeral pace, correct. Cairn 44.

*Unit hand:* CONDITION ON RECEIPT: declined to file cleanly. Call-sign produced once at the graveside, a half-step flat, immediately disavowed by every witness present. Chalk count of this cairn was pre-correct by two days. This unit enters, under protest, a new category of deceased: **RETIRED, LISTENING.**

**BAND ZERO.** *[No cairn. Registered here at last, after forty years of warden requests denied on grounds of bookkeeping and granted now on grounds of politeness. Band zero broadcasts one long vowel, pre-Delay to present, without pausing.]* — *Warden hand: the breath in. When it stops, this book becomes a diary.*
