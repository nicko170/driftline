*Recovered from the pannier strongbox of bike 07 at the ridge crest, wax seals intact. Nothing was taken. That is the part the Ledger Office cannot file, and the part I cannot stop reading. Copied fair by hand. — K.*

## Driftline manifest — run 17, north loop

**COURIER:** Davenant, R. (senior, storm-rated, cleared for hum-tolerant freight)
**BIKE:** 07 ("older than the Creed and twice as stubborn" — her annotation on the hire line)

Item 1 — Survey staples, crated: flour, lamp oil, one (1) harmonica reed. CONSIGNOR: Salt Guild, Saltmouth. CONSIGNEE: Windspine survey camp. MASS: 9 kg. SEAL: manifest wax, Guild ochre. **Fee: 40 cr. Wholly unremarkable.**

Item 2 — Mail bag, sealed: fourteen letters, one parcel marked CROCKERY — DO NOT TIP, which per the bag slip contains a *hat*. CONSIGNEE: turbine crews, row. **Fee: 25 cr.**

Item 3 — Personal. No mass. No seal. Her handwriting, level, unhurried: *"for whoever's counting."* **Fee: none listed. The Guild audited this line twice and found nothing to charge.**

## Route slips (hog-hide, pencil, her hand)

**Slip A — Saltmouth to survey camp.** Departure marked 06:10, arrival marked 08:40, pencil asterisk: *"stopped to let a dust-fox finish crossing. Worth it. Total: +3 min, one small perfect morning."* Camp countersign: *received, staples sound, courier ate beans, courier complained about beans, courier asked for more beans.*

**Slip B — survey camp to turbine row.** Departure 09:05. Mail delivered at the row by 09:50; crew chief's mark: *courier saluted the machines. The machines — I am writing this under protest and also because it is true — saluted* back*.* CRC lift noted: 10:15, crest bound.

**Slip C — ridge crest to Saltmouth (return).** Blank. Not torn. *Blank.* The pencil is still in the slip's loop, which Rill never left sheathed — she called it a holstered sidearm and drew it to settle bar bets. The return fee was prepaid in Saltmouth, which she also never did. "A courier without a return plan," she told me once, "is just a delivery that hasn't landed."

## The gap

Gauge station time-mark: her transponder crossed the crest line at 10:41.

Beacon resumes — no. Beacon does not resume. What resumes, at 11:22, is a forty-one-minute blank with a single correction overlaid, in ink, on the pencil grid: the crest waypoint struck through, and in its place a bearing that doesn't point anywhere on any chart the Guild will admit to printing. The ink is teal. Rill carried two pencils and a stub of grease marker. *Nobody on the ridge stocks teal ink. Nobody anywhere stocks teal ink. The Guild checked. The Guild checked like it was a debt.*

Under the bearing, one line, small, calm as a bar tab:

*"Route adjusted. Don't follow the ink, junior. Follow the count."*

There is no count on the slip. There is no count anywhere in the strongbox. Logbook copies of this record are stamped PENDING and the stamps, per the clerk who applied them, will not stay dry.

*I rode the bearing anyway. Junior moves fast; the desert moves always. Ask me at the garage what I found at the end of the teal line. Bring beans. — K.*
