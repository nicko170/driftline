*Posted by order of Brinemaster Ogo in all Guild exchanges. Abridged for readability; the full charter is 214 pages and mostly about salt taxonomy.*

**Article One — Purpose.** The Salt Guild exists to ensure that goods, water, credit, and news cross the Glass Desert in an orderly fashion, and that someone sensible counts them. Civilization is defined, for charter purposes, as a place where a parcel's weight is written down before anybody gets emotional about it.

**Article Two — Motto.** *Everything crosses. Everything's counted.*

**Article Three — Membership.** Any settlement, caravan, or free trader may join by contributing one working scale, one sealed ledger, and one piece of gossip verified independently by two members. The gossip requirement is not a joke. Information rots faster than saltfish; a Guild that cannot trade rumors cannot trade at all.

**Article Four — The Manifest.** All cargo is entered in the manifest before movement. Cargo without a manifest line does not, charter-legally, exist, and must be referred to the Ledger Office under Form 22-B, where it will be filed under "anomalous," "philosophy," or "the Choir's problem," at the factor's discretion.

**Article Five — Neutrality of Couriers.** The Driftline couriers are chartered neutral. The Guild does not own them, the Choir does not bless them, the Reclaimers do not strip them. Any faction interfering with a courier forfeits manifest privileges for one season, which historically has ended every dispute it has ever been applied to, once.

**Article Six — Debt.** Debts are honored, tallied, and occasionally forgiven on the debtor's hundredth anniversary, whichever comes second.

**Sealed and posted** in the 193rd year of the Delay. Countersigned: Factor T. Cho, Saltmouth exchange. *(Marginal note, initialed T.C.: "Someone forged a name in tomorrow's ledger today. If you are that someone: excellent penmanship, and we need to talk.")*
