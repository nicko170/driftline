*Extract regulation 4: pages of the Great Ledger may be copied for instruction provided sums are exact, names are respectful, and the doodles are preserved. The doodles are load-bearing. — Office of the Counterweight*

## The page (Saltmouth column, current year of the Delay)

**KETCH, garage-keeper of the Driftline workshed.** Owes: 340 cr, paint pigment, one (1) engine hoist out on indefinite loan to the road at large. Collateral: none accepted; offered "my word and a torque wrench"; the auditor accepted the wrench and returned the word as change. *Margin: a wrench, drawn well. Brinemaster's hand.*

**BAHRO, O., water-song pensioner.** Owes: 12 cr, tea. Debt age: thirty-one years. Interest status: forgiven annually at the Delay's anniversary, re-incurred annually the following morning, both parties satisfied. *Margin: a teacup, steaming. Long-running. The steam is a different clerk each year; the cup is always the same cup.*

**MARROWS, J., racer (see also: standing wagers, sub-ledger 19).** Owes: 610 cr, entry fees advanced against winnings that were "a certainty, legally speaking." Collateral: one (1) racing canopy, shot with noon light, surrendered gracefully and re-won from the Guild at cards before the wax dried. *Margin: the canopy drawn on fire. Clerk's note: "accurate."*

**PEMMY, weather goat, Guild desk.** Owes: nothing. Owed *to*: one (1) affectionate goat, tendered as collateral by a herder in the year 6 of the Delay, never redeemed, now staff. The Ledger records her under livestock; the Ledger's staff record themselves, in chalk on the page's edge, under *hers*.

**DAVENANT, R., courier.** Owes: 290 cr, medical wrap and one (1) storm-lamp. *Entry struck through in a single line so gentle it reads as tucking-in.* Paid in full, whereabouts the payment. Note beneath, Counterweight's hand: "This line stays struck. Some debts we cancel; some we adjourn. The lamp was hers. It is lit wherever she is counting." *Margin: two hundred and eleven tally marks, crossed in fives. Nobody has explained the count. Nobody has been asked to.*

**CHOIR OF THE FIRST NOTE, the, vs. THE GUILD.** Mutual, circular, older than the bridge. Owed to the Choir: one promise concerning rain. Owed by the Choir: one explanation concerning rain. Both parties file annually and neither delivers. *Margin: a small cloud, raining upward.*

**VARGA, A., courier (second-hand machine, serviceable).** Bond of indentured goodwill: **8,000 cr**, standard courier's purchase, payable at the exchange in any weather. Collateral: the machine itself, valued in the ledger at scrap and by its rider at everything. Consent of debtor: *PRESUMED*. The stamp is manifest wax. The wax was inspected while still warm and found dry. No clerk at the exchange admits to the hand. The stamp is in the hand everyone uses for bad news, executed here on what the rest of the column insists, quietly and in pencil, is the good kind.

**Margin, foot of page:** *a little humming crate, drawn with care, drawn twice — the second time with a door.*
