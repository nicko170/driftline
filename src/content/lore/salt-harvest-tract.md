*Printed by the Flats-Edge Growers' Circle on the back of a voided Guild manifest, because the Circle does not buy paper and does not forgive paper either. Distributed free. Reading is compulsory in the eyes of the Circle.*

## How the harvest works, since nobody asks

Salt is not mined here. Salt is *farmed*, and the difference is patience. We flood the Old Pan at dawn from the tower ration, we wait for the sun to take the water back, and by dusk the pan has grown a crust you can read like a letter. Pink crust means brine-shrimp bloom and a sweet grade. White is table grade. The thin teal sheeting at the pan's west lip is fused silica, glass the old burns laid down, and it grows back slowest of all. A good farmer harvests glass once a year and apologises to the pan for a week.

Fused silica, for the record, tastes like the memory of lightning. You are not supposed to taste it. Everyone tastes it once.

## The Enemies of the Harvest, in order of salt lost

1. **Wind.** Undefeated. Comes at night, takes the crust's top millimetre, pays nothing.
2. **Dew.** A thief that works for the wind. Condenses on the crust, carries it away grain by grain. We have filed complaints with the sky. Pending.
3. **Guild factors.** Price us against salt brought by skyship from nowhere, then complain our salt is dear. Our salt is dear because our salt is *ours*. The Guild calls this attitude. The Circle calls it arithmetic.
4. **Hover-bikes.** Your wake flattens the crust's reading-lines, courier. Ride the marked causeway, which exists, which we painted, which you can see from the sky you drive down out of.
5. **Children.** Ours. Chronic. Unfixable. Listed here for completeness and in the hope that shame will do what fences cannot.

## On terms

We sell to the Guild because the Guild has the scales, and we resent the Guild because the Guild has the scales. This is a stable arrangement and has outlasted every alternative proposed by people who have never lifted a brine rake. Honest weight, prompt water ration, and no poetry at the exchange window — meet those three and the Circle will trade with anyone alive. It has, in fact, traded with almost everyone alive, and one buyer who hummed and was arguably several people inside a radio. That account is still open. That account stays open until somebody explains the humming.
