*As read over the Saltmouth harbour wireless at second bell, copied from the booth slate by the apprentice on duty. Items marked [margin] are the harbourmaster's pencil, which is his whole personality and one good eye.*

---

## Bulletin of the day, all piers

**Flour.** Sixteen flats of third-grade stone flour, Keel Town consignment, dock four. Two flats burst in transit; the crane crew has been voted Custodians of the Biscuit and will distribute at dusk. [margin: bring your own bowl, last year's arguments filed under TRAGIC]

**Mooring fees.** Due. All of them. The skyship *Patient Gull* is reminded that "the mooring is a friendship" is not a currency the dock accepts, regardless of how beautifully Captain Dow phrased it. [margin: it WAS beautifully phrased]

**Water.** Charter rates hold. The charter holds. Hold yourselves accordingly.

**Lost.** One dog, answers to Biscuit, which has caused the expected problem with the flour. Reward: one biscuit. The irony is free. [margin: dog found. dog is fine. dog voted himself Custodian]

**Arrived, no manifest.** One crate. Mass within courier tolerance. No sender of record, no receiver of record, no lot number that resolves in any ledger this office can shake. Serial plate blank in a way that plates are not. It hums, faintly, at a pitch several handlers describe as "polite." One handler described it as "known to me," requested reassignment, and has been granted it. [margin: no further questions]

**Assigned.** Route of the above in accordance with standing order for orphan freight: posted to the Driftline board under seal, first qualified courier, standard fragile handling, standard non-disclosure. The non-disclosure is not standard. The non-disclosure is what is standard now. [margin: no further questions]

**Inquiry.** The Salt Guild asks whether the office has anything to declare. The office declares: flour, a dog, fees. [margin: no further questions]

**Inquiry.** The Choir asks whether the office has heard anything. The office has not. The office is paid in hearing and declares none surplus. [margin: no further questions]

**Inquiry.** The Reclaimers did not ask anything, which this office notes with the same care it notes fog. [margin: no questions at all]

**Weather.** Season opens early, probably. See the goat.

**Closing.** Bulletin ends. The harbourmaster reminds all piers that a bulletin is a list of what is ordinary, and that a day is ordinary by decree of the office, and that the day's decree stands, and that the crate is on the board, and that the board is where freight goes to become somebody else's weather.

*Second bell past. The booth slate was wiped. The pencil margins were copied first, as is custom, because the margins are the part that keeps.*
