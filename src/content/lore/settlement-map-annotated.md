*Source: survey lithograph, Okafor-Bligh Colonial Works, printed year minus-9, titled PROSPECTIVE HABITATIONS, KESSA-9 REV. C. Hung in the Saltmouth exchange hall until the frame was needed for glass; now folded in the reserve drawer, amended in blade and graphite by whoever last carried it. Couriers annotate maps the way critics annotate poems — extensively, angrily, in the margins of survival.*

## The dots that lived

**Site 7 — "Marina."** Printed nearly touching the sea. The sea is ninety kilometres that-a-way and made of salt. Knife amendment, old: *SALTMOUTH. It worked anyway. Don't ask.* The tide line on the lithograph is still the best joke in the town.

**Site 12 — forever unnamed.** Amendment: *WINDSPINE. Wind DOES farm here. Printing was right. Gloat noted.* Under it, later: *storm gauge works. goats work. we're fine.*

**Site 19 — "Crater Meadow."** Amendment, careful, in lamp-black ink rather than a knife, which is how you know the Choir did it: *THE HOLLOW. The meadow is asleep, not cancelled.* Below, in graphite: *fair.*

**Site 23 — "Uplift Anchorage."** Amendment: *SKYDOCKS. Money lives here. Wind answers to it. Moorage posted quarterly, argued hourly.*

## The dots that didn't

**Site 4.** Scratched out so hard the paper tore; under the tear: *bitter flats. gone in one night. the wall kept the name.* Cairn symbol. Leave a stone.

**Sites 9, 10, 11, 14.** Each scratched; a single hand toured the whole map once, adding the same four words to each: *tried. dried. cairned. left.* On site 14, the hand broke pattern: *sorry.* Nothing else. The exchange keeps the fold flat over that corner.

**Site 16 — "Grand Junction."** Never scratched out. Amended with *YARDS* and then, in Reclaimer orange: *everything here is useful. including the name. we kept the junction, lost the grand, found it again in pieces.* Cairn symbol crossed out, replaced with a spark. The only upbeat revision on the sheet.

## The legend, annotated

The lithograph's printed legend promises *water within 5 km of all habitations.* Framed beneath it, newer ink, four words, unanimous across forty hands: **annotation not required. lol.**

At the map's raw edge, outside the printed border, one more dot, drawn in, unnamed. Under it, recent, in courier pencil: *this one's not on the map yet. keep it that way a while.* No blade has touched it. That too is an amendment.
