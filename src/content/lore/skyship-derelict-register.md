*Nailed beside Dockmaster Vane's hut, laminated in manifest wax, updated in whatever colour ink the quarter supplied. Rule of the register, in Vane's own hand across the top: "A ship is derelict when her captain says so, her crew agrees, or ten years pass. Not before. Not if you are only bored of waiting."*

## Hull register, current page

**PALE AMARANTH**, guild barge, last master: Capt. Eunu Quill (retired, sells rope, does not want to discuss it). Aboard, per inventory: four hundred chair legs, one chair, a piano nobody will carry down. Claim: Guild, uncontested, *unwanted*.

**SECOND BREAKFAST**, pleasure yawl. Last master: unknown; logbook is a menu. Aboard: galley in working order. Crew of fir cats, self-appointed, fed, ferocious. Claim: the cats. The cats have prevailed in arbitration twice.

**LONG DIVORCE**, ore barge, moored stern-first in year 41 and never corrected. Aboard: ballast, grievances, a horn that sounds itself on cold nights. Claim: contested between the Reclaimers (she is *scrap*) and her last engineer (she is *resting*). Engineer wins on points; the horn testifies.

**VERITY OF CHAINS**, survey cutter. Aboard: charts marked mostly *here too, nothing* in a fond hand. Claim: Driftline historical interest, which is a claim that costs nothing and is worth about that.

## The one that left

**EVEN JOVE**, grain hauler, last master: Capt. Sible Woon, deceased in office, year 96. Listed derelict year 106. Struck from moorage year 111 — written in the register as **DEPARTED**, signed by Vane's predecessor's predecessor. Addendum, year 118, different hand: *Nobody cast her off. Nobody was paid to. The mast-book has her dues paid through departure. Paid by whom.* Addendum, year 130, third hand, deeper cut, as if written slowly: *The yard kids say she rose on a clear dawn with no pilot aboard, turned her nose to the glass hills, and went wherever even Jove goes. The winch crews say the kids are liars. Both crews drink together. Make of it what you will.* — Inspection advised. Inspection declined, per Dockmaster Devanna Shore: *the mooring is fine. The story is load-bearing.*
