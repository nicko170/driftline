*Recovered from the mesa-top mooring house, skydocks, in a drawer the Guild had invoiced as 'emptied (contents: atmosphere)'. The log is a bound greasewood-board folio; handwriting is the moorage master's throughout, steady until the last eleven pages.*

---

## Fleet list, as ruled (condensed)

BERTHS 1–4: cargo lifters, Guild charter. Status all: *on rotation*.
BERTHS 5–9: line haulers, Combine colours. Status: *awaiting cargo*.
BERTH 10: the *Palmer-Dexter*, survey dirigible. Status: *late*. (Margin, same hand: *late is not lost. late is a kind of punctual, westward.*)
BERTH 11: charter ship, unlisted owner. Status: *paid through the spring.* (Margin: *whose spring*)
BERTH 12: kept empty on principle. Principles: good.

## Entries, final two seasons (selected)

*Second season, midterm.* Three departures, one arrival. Arrival was a training rig with student crews who waved at the desert like it waved back. It doesn't. Noted for the record.

*Seven bells into the blow.* Berth 6 snapped its storm-line and swung. We kept her. The crew want it logged that "the ship apologised." Logged.

*Wire received.* Funding review. Language: "pause pending resumption." The master's copy reads, in the margin, *say the other word*. The wire office does not say the other word on wires.

*Berth 10 update.* Still late. Re-marked the expected-return tally. There is no argument in this log about it. (Previous season there was some argument in this log about it.)

*Last twelve ships out, in order:* confirmation difficult; winds. This master kept the lamps lit through the month anyway, because moorage is what the lamps are *for*, not what the ships are.

*Closing entry, undated.* All berths struck, all slings coiled, one visitor's flagles up because the skyships like the look of it. If you are the resumption, welcome back: berth 12 is empty on principle, and the principle survived. — M. (master, moorage, mesa)

*Marginalia, much later, chalk, artist unknown but suspected to be Keel Town:* still late. bell's ringing anyway.
