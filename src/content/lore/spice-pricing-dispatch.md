## The board at dusk

**Prices, then. The market opens where it always opens: at the point of maximum regret.**

**Salt.** Food grade holds at four the tile. Industrial steady. Decorative salt — a real category, apparently, and may the desert forgive us all — up on Keel Town wedding season, two betrothals and a reconciliation. The reconciliation is priced separately, like dessert, and worth it.

**Water.** Charter rates hold. The Charter holds. Hold yourselves accordingly. This desk notes that "hold" has now appeared in this sentence three times, which is more than some couriers.

**Cinder pepper.** Up, then down, then up again, on news that this year's crop is "fiery," news sourced entirely from the man selling it. Futures traders are reminded that heat is not a valuation model. The Church of the Obvious has issued a statement; the statement is *ow*.

**Gossip.** Bullish. Trading heavy, well above water, for the third dusk running. Analysts attribute the bubble to a certain shipment described in manifests as — and this desk reads exactly — nothing. Nothing is, at present, the best-performing commodity on Kessa-9. Congratulations to everyone holding nothing.

## The item that wasn't

**Item.** One crate. Cubic. Mass within courier tolerance. Opening price — the Guild offers its usual figure, which is a cough. The Choir offers reverence, which it cannot make change for. The Reclaimers offer *prompt collection*, which is not a bid, though this desk admires the confidence and has locked the booth.

*[bell]*

**Listeners are informed** that this desk has been asked — by letter; the letter had no sender; the letter nonetheless found the booth, which is doing wonders for booth security morale — to strike the previous forty seconds. Struck. Listeners will please forget the item they did not hear, concerning the crate that is not for sale at any price, on account of being, per every ledger this desk can shake, not there.

## Close of light

**Weather futures.** Fictional. **Goat futures:** bearish. The goat has seen something and is being a professional about it, which this desk finds frankly more alarming than any wall on the ridge. **Soap:** none. Again. If you have soap, you are weather now.

**Horizon cells** hold steady — older than the price of anything, humming at a pitch several brokers described as "judging me personally." This desk does not trade in cells. This desk barely trades in judgment, and then only evenings.

**And that is the market, Kessa-9:** everything priced, nothing for sale that matters, dusk — as always — free of charge and holding value. Spend it before the stars come in. The Standard will be here at close of light. The Standard is always here.

*[bell, fainter, as if apologising]*
