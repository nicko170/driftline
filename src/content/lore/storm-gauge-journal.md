*Journal of keeper Evren Ost, Windspine Gauge Station, the tripod on the north saddle between Turbines 3 and 4. Station duties: read the master gauge, log the gust bands, key the numbers to Saltmouth twice daily, do not editorialize. Editorializing below is the keeper's own and the Guild disavows it warmly.*

---

## Readings, verbatim, editorializing included

**Season-minus 22.** Base wind 14, gust band 22–26. Normal. Pemmy's desk says the season starts on time. Pemmy's desk says a lot of things with a whole goat behind them. Bought the season's lamp oil anyway. Trust is a fuel and I am rationing.

**Season-minus 15.** Base 19, gusts to 34. Copper in the dawn sky, that thin kind, like the desert trying a colour on. Hala at Turbine 9 keys that I'm jumpy. I key back that her tower is jumpy. We are both right. That is the job.

**Season-minus 9.** Base 24. The gauge drum has a voice at 24 — a low double-note, something between a hum and a complaint. Heard it all last season. Logged it as BEARING WEAR, which it is. Logged it under that, in pencil, as IT KNOWS MY NAME, which the Guild does not need to hear about.

**Season-minus 4.** Pemmy forecast in: SEASON OPENS EARLY, PROBABLY. The probably is doing a lot of work for one word. The Choir came up the saddle with lamps to ask the tripod what it dreams. I told them it dreams in columns of numbers. They wrote that down like it was scripture, which, fine, everything here is scripture to somebody.

**Season 1.** Opened. First wall sighted off the southern flats at dusk, a Walker, polite as far as walls go. Keyed Saltmouth: SEASON OPEN, STANDING ORDERS, GOOD LUCK EVERYONE. Hala keyed back an oilcan icon and one word: WITNESSED. That's how keepers say amen.

**Season 5.** Base 31. Gust band 40–55 and *climbing between readings*, which the gauge is not supposed to do, which I am logging under CALIBRATION because the alternative word is *growing*.

**Season 8.** Double-note in the drum all night now. It syncs with whatever the crates in the freight queue are doing — there is one down there humming along like it paid for the lesson. Couriers keep asking the ridge what's wrong. The ridge asks back.

**Season 11.** Constants are a comfort we invented. Logged the gust band and did not look at it twice. Lamp oil shares with whoever reads this after me. Read the gauge twice daily. Do not editorialize. It goes faster.

*The entries continue in this manner and then, briefly, in a taller stack of unopened oil tins. Season total, walls: above average. Season total, editorializing: below the keeper's fear threshold, which is exactly what the journal is for.*
