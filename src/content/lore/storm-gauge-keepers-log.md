*Kept at the storm-gauge station on the Windspine crest; entries in the keeper's hand, season margins in the Guild weather desk's hand, and two entries in a third hand that neither admits to.*

---

## Entries, one storm season, Windspine crest

**Season open.** Gauge glassed and true; tether re-tarred; the tea situation remains a situation. Wind from the spine, steady, honest. Barometer says *weather*. It has said *weather* for forty years. Good.

**Day nine.** Wall SE, speed twenty-two, came in early against the desk's own almanac. Logged as early. The almanac margin notes that the log is wrong. The log notes the almanac.

**Day twenty-one.** Second wall, bearing wrong by the compass of every previous entry, season on record said calm. The goats at the desk predicted it; the instruments did not. Keeps being mentioned. Keep being right.

**Day forty-four.** Dust shows a periodicity of two-point-one hours, exactly, like the desert is pacing. Suggested joke: it is keeping an appointment. Laughed alone. Logged anyway.

**Day sixty.** THE ENTRY IN QUESTION. Barometer needle rose against pressure, settled on a mark with no graduation, and — this keeper states for the record, with the instruments' full legal jeopardy — spelled, by tick and rest, the word STILL. Then it rained somewhere east of east, where nothing lives to want rain. Did not initial this entry. It initials itself, if you watch.

**Season close.** Weather desk copyist's summary: "irregular sequences; recommend goat consultation." Keeper's counter-summary: "irregular is a kind of regular, if you hold still long enough. The goats knew. The barometer knows. The calendar is a clerk with strong opinions."
