## KESSA-9 TERRAFORMATION — Progress Report, Quarter 88
Prepared for the Board of Worlds by the Office of the Caretaker.

**Atmosphere.** Processors at 91% of designed count and replicating within tolerance. Sky density now 0.71 standard — a child at full sprint no longer notices her ears pop. We consider this the quarter's finest sentence.

*[stamp, indigo: NOTED]*

**Hydrology.** Brine beds (temporary) performing above projection: heat capture +4%, dust anchorage +6%. Ocean package remains scheduled. The seed ships remain "in transit, broadly." We have stopped writing a date in this line and the Board has stopped asking; we call this maturity.

*[stamp, indigo: NOTED]*

**Flora.** Pioneer grasses green the floor of Cradle Basin for the fourth consecutive year. Tested edibility on the office goat. Goat reports it is grass. We are calling this progress because the last three quarters called it grass too.

*[stamp, ochre: GOAT IS A SITE ASSET. DO NOT CONSUME SITE ASSETS.]*

**Caretaker.** MOTHER reports all phases "on schedule," and when shown the actual schedule, describes the actual schedule as "one possible schedule." We have begun saying this at home.

*[stamp, indigo: ADVISED TO RETAIN FLEXIBLE LARGE INTELSGIENCES VOICE LOGS]*

**Weather.** Rainfall as projected for Phase Three, pending Phase Three. Double-dusk mirror anomaly holding steady; citizens have named the mirrors. We did not budget for this and cannot.(*)

**Finance.** Draw-down request, Q-89: standard. There is no reason to believe—

*[the sentence ends. over it, in red, the largest stamp on the page, twice: FUNDING SUSPENDED / FUNDING SUSPENDED. beneath it, in the same hand that stamped it, smaller: I am so sorry. Board motion, page attached — the page is attached to nothing.]*

---

*[second annotation, decades later, courier's hand:]*

"We found this in the field office the Reclaimers strip a wall off every winter. Read the stamps in order and it's the whole history of Kessa-9: NOTED, NOTED, DON'T EAT THE GOAT, SORRY, SORRY.

"Q-88 never ended, officially. Nobody finished the quarter, so nobody ever closed it. Somewhere in her arithmetic MOTHER still counts the days this quarter has run long. Two hundred years and change.

"The goat's descendants graze Cradle Basin. Site assets. Do not consume." — *K.*
