*Local 9 ratified this service in Year 30 of the Delay, after a loader was scrapped quietly and three members grieved for a week and could not say why. Machinery outlives most of us. The local holds that anything that carried you deserves a sentence over it. Aunt Vertex keeps the copy. Aunt Vertex will not be outlived, but the local notes this is "in the hands of the bearings."*

## The service, in full

The crew gathers at the machine. The senior member strikes the casing once — "respectfully, with a ring you can hear." Anyone may speak. The liturgy does not require words, only that nobody be loading or welding while they are said.

**Vertex:** *"Who kept this one?"*
**Assembly:** *"We kept this one, and it kept us."*
**Vertex:** *"What did it carry?"*
**Assembly:** *"It carried the work. It carried us home out of weather. It carried its own weight longer than anyone asked."*
**Vertex:** *"What remains?"*
**Assembly:** *"The good steel, the honest copper, and every lesson it took off our hands the hard way. Strip it with thanks. Nothing true is wasted here."*

Then the machine's name — Local 9 names everything, including the compressor three members had a standing feud with — is entered in the yard book, and retired parts are chalked with its initial so they remember where they came from. Spares installed elsewhere are considered to be *visiting*.

## Appended account, against the wishes of the officiant

In Storm Year 41 the crew retired the yard's vending unit, a machine of no romance and two products — lukewarm fizz and a soup of disputed temperature. A motion was made that the unit had *carried the crew* in the only sense that mattered at 3 a.m. on audit nights. Vertex ruled the motion in order and conducted the full service. The strike on the casing returned, witnesses agree, "the best ring of the year, and the machine hadn't run since Frost."

The unit's initial — V, uncontested — now appears on two fuse boxes, a kettle, and the Crusher's regulator housing. Vertex says the lesson is that the liturgy should be applied with discernment. The crew says the lesson is that the liturgy should be applied. The yard book records only: *carried: the night shifts. Remains: still deciding.* The local considers this the service working as intended.
