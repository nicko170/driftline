## Finding one: the press exists

Saltmouth's print-shop runs a Verdantine GP-4 manifest press, pre-Delay, rack-fed, which has had no power feed since Year One of the Delay. Its horizon cell is a museum piece — Sister Counterweight's predecessor logged it *decorative* — and the shop's windows were boarded before my grandmother learned fractions. The press produced, last season, one (1) manifest. Sealed, waxed, correctly formatted. The crate it describes is on a courier's rack and will not stop humming.

## Finding two: the toner

Assay, three passes (I did not trust the first): pigment stock matches Guild issue **of a formulation discontinued one hundred and eleven years ago**. The fixative contains fused glass particulates consistent with the Glassroad burn. The wax is our wax, current season. Conclusion, first draft: "a hoax of unusual thoroughness." Conclusion, revised, struck through: "[illegible]." Conclusion, final: *the document is new and its ingredients are old, and I do not currently have a column for that.*

## Finding three: the timestamp

The manifest's print-head registers cycle counts. This head reports its **two hundred and third cycle, dated Tuesday**. No year. Just Tuesday. The press's maintenance log — recovered from the boarded shop, mildewed, legible — ends two centuries ago with the entry "still operational," which is a phrase I have previously only seen used about the Yards' crusher, and I am beginning to understand it is not a report but a *diagnosis*.

The shop floor showed no footprints in the dust. The dust showed a single clean rectangle where something sat recently and was lifted *straight up*.

## Finding four: the drawer

Cross-reference complete. The same press appears, by serial, in the Year One ledger, where its last billed job is listed as "cradle stock, dormant, paired." Paired. I have read the Reclaimer bench notes the courier calls gossip. A sleeper and a nudge. We have been auditing a *nudge*.

## Recommendation

None. Recommendations assume a lever. I can offer only an observation, and I enter it in the working file because observations are what new drawers are for:

**The machine had no power. The machine has never needed power. We keep saying "printed" because the alternative is "delivered," and the alternative to that is a word the Choir says on purpose and I refuse to learn.**

Filed under Tuesday.

*The auditor's name has been sealed by her own request. She continues to direct the actuarial division, entire, and has added a line item to every Guild policy: "events of meaning — see bench rate." — S.C.*
