*Driftline Training Annex 12 — Weather. The Guild desk at Saltmouth issues forecasts via a goat standing on an engraved brass chart. This annex teaches you to read that desk the way you read a storm door: from experience, quickly, and with respect. Compiled by the clerks; approved by the goat; the goat approved by standing on the draft.*

## Reading the output, for riders

The desk posts one of four conditions each dawn, in paint, at the exchange steps:

**STOOD** — the named region is fair. Ride it. Do not dawdle out of gratitude; Pemmy does not issue second opinions on a day she's already stood for.

**STAMPED, once through four** — a wall is coming, one stamp per wall, before the thaw. The stamp location is approximate to about a half-basin. The stamp *time* is approximate to almost nothing. Budget your run as though the wall has already read your schedule, because — third clerk's note, struck through and reinstated four times — *it behaves as though it has.*

**LAID DOWN** — the velvet forecast. All riders shelter. The desk does not negotiate, the Guild does not refund, and couriers who ride a laid-down day lose their number. Not their life, note. Their *number*. The line stops answering them first.

**GONE INSIDE** — rare. Four-stamp days excepted, the goat has left the chart for the loft interior only five times in eleven seasons. All five times it rained. On Kessa-9. The clerks paint this condition in a colour kept in a locked drawer.

## The statistics (unpublished)

The clerks maintain a private ledger of desk accuracy versus the dead satellite archive, the Choir's static readings, and the Reclaimers' barometric hull — an old pressure vessel Boss Pyke swears by and names after her grandmother. Over eleven seasons: **Pemmy 96%, hull 61%, static 58%, satellites 0% (deceased).** The ledger is unpublished because — head clerk's marginalia — *publishing a number like that about a goat creates appetites the office cannot feed.*

## The five greatest forecasts, as ranked at relay fires

**5.** The year she stamped three on Windspine's *chart image* of the ridge, twenty hours before the ridge's only historic wall. First recorded instance of the desk forecasting a place instead of a region.

**4.** The wedding wall. Stamped twice at dawn; the Guild refused to postpone a counting-house wedding at noon. The wall arrived during the vows, politely, the way walls don't. Witnesses agree the goat attended, uninvited, and stood in the cake's lee.

**3.** The laid-down day nobody believed — the basin was *glass-still*, sunrise to noon. The wall stood up out of a clear sky at dusk like something that had been waiting politely behind the horizon. Four saved caravans. The clerks get letters about this one every year. The goat gets dried apricots.

**2.** The gone-inside Wednesday — third of the five. Rain for an hour. Keel Town held an unscheduled Swing.
