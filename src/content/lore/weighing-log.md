*From the working log of Sister Counterweight, Auditor-Titular, Salt Guild Ledger Office, Saltmouth. The log is a bound stack of waxed leaves, each entry in the same level hand, each ruled with the three columns the Office prefers: WHAT, HOW MUCH, and OTHERWISE. Excerpts copied with permission, which was granted as follows: "Copy what you like. The interesting page will not be in it." It is, reader. It is page 203.*

## The protocol, as recorded

*Entry directive, leaf 1:* Weigh salt by the beam, souls by the beam, and letters of credit by the beam, in that order only. Dust the pan before souls; souls take offence at grit, which registers. Record intention where legible. Intention is denser than it looks. Breathe out. The subject breathes out. Everyone breathes out. It is a small office and we are all in it together.

## A season of unremarkable entries

*Leaf 47, WHAT:* quatro salt, blue grade. *HOW MUCH:* 40.1 tare. *OTHERWISE:* courier attempted to be weighed WITH the salt "to save time." Time is not a numbered asset in this Office. Corrected.

*Leaf 88, WHAT:* one soul, debtor, new bond, A. V. *HOW MUCH:* as expected. *OTHERWISE:* the beam hesitated before settling, which it does for the stubborn and the unsupervised. Filed under stubborn. Recommended for courier work, where stubborn is load-bearing.

*Leaf 112, WHAT:* one soul, guild factor, inspection tour. *HOW MUCH:* declined to print. *OTHERWISE:* beam read *insured.* The beam has never before used the word. The factor left a gratuity. The gratuity has been weighed and entered as a brief warm day.

*Leaf 140, WHAT:* letters of credit, bulk, choir negotiators. *HOW MUCH:* elegant. *OTHERWISE:* the numbers sang, faintly, at the harmonic the Hollow uses for the Sleeping Garden. Do the numbers always sing. Awaiting the Office's answer. The Office is myself. Still awaiting.

## Page 203

*[The page carries no WHAT. The entry is dated the night the sealed crate passed through the exchange, listed as unmanifested, listed by nobody, signed by a hand the ledgers have never matched.]*

*HOW MUCH:* —

And underneath, where the number should stand, the pen pressed hard enough to bruise the leaf:

*the beam came to rest on BOTH SIDES*

*[Below that, nothing for three lines. Then, in the same level hand, only smaller:]*

*It did not weigh heavy. It weighed* present. *Like a debt that has already decided you will pay it. Like a door accounting for the house around it. The beam holds the reading now. It has held it for eleven days. I have stopped calibrating. Calibration assumes the world is on the other pan.*

*[Final line:]*

*Transfer request, formal, any distance considered. The Office declines. The Office is myself. Request denied. See memorandum regarding counting backwards.*

*The leaf following is blank. Every leaf after is blank, to the end of the book. The log continues, of course — Sister Counterweight's hand appears on later waxed stock, rule-lined, level, unshaken — but this particular book was closed that night and never ruled again, as if the Office had decided, unanimously and alone, that some things are filed by shutting them.*
