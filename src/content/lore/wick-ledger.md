*From the wick ledger, back pages, Lamp Rest. The front pages are inventory — oil in, salt counted twice, biscuits — but the back of the book belongs to the rim. Ninety-one lamps, entered nightly by whoever's hands were keeping them. The entries below are copied by Verger Sann, current hands, who was asked for the book and gave the interesting parts "in self-defence."*

## Entries, selected across two hundred years

**Year 7.** *Lamps: 12 burning, rim unfinished, crater unroofed, everything cold.* Kept 12 lit. It is enough. It has to be enough; there is nobody else. — *hands freeze to the pen some nights. Bought a second blanket with the lamp-oil money. The lamps said nothing. The lamps are lamps. Forgive the entry.*

**Year 61.** *Lamps: 44.* New keeper, third of the line, writes: *the rim now reads from below like a crown. The old keeper's rule stands: a wick catches for patience, not horsepower. Counted twice. The counting is the prayer; the prayer is the counting; the ledger does not care which it is called.*

**Year 103.** *Lamps: 71.* Storm season entry, in a shaking hand: *lamp 3 (west arc) is proud. It burns brighter when watched. I have decided this is permitted. I have also decided not to tell the temple. Temples make doctrines; vergers make light.*

**Year 117.** *[Entry ends mid-sentence:]* "The rim is lit and the wind has stopped and the silence is so exactly the shape of the crater that I think someone is about to" — *[the pen line trails off the page. The book resumes on the next leaf, eighty years on, new ink, new hand:]* "— speak. Found this unfinished. The notebook of my predecessor kept her sentence warm. Entry continued in her honour. Lamps: 88."

**Year 190.** *Lamps: 91. The ring is whole.* Current line of hands begins. Eleven sulking, on average; sulking means burning low until visited, which is either a draught fault or loneliness, and the stores issue for both is a walk around the rim. *Every lamp named. The names are inventory codes written backwards. No one has noticed. The lamps have, evidently, forgiven me.*

**Year 203, stores addendum.** *Received: one (1) tin, lamp oil, old blue grade, no sender, no manifest, seal intact, cold to the touch.* Delivery freight paid in advance by a name the exchange has no ledger page for. Hums faintly at the frequency the proud lamp prefers. Placed at the back of stores, where it will keep. Keeping is what stores are for. The top floor will be told when the top floor asks in the correct tense.

## The latest entry

**Last night.** *Lamps: 91, lit before full dark, first time this season.* Rider-provided; courier walked the ring with the satchel and held still at each arc like a professional, which is to say like a believer. Eleven sulks resolved; the proud one unctuous; the horn's lamp spoke two words, filed under weather by the Static-Warden and under *discrepancy* by me.

Totals: oil steady, salt counted twice, dusk one short.

We don't finish things at Lamp Rest. We keep them. The difference is the whole of the job.
