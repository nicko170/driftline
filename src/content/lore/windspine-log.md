*Log of keeper Hala Osti, Turbine 9, Windspine Ridge. Kept per Guild standing order on a slate chained to the nacelle table. Recovered by courier, first calm week after season's end. Some pages sand-scoured; what remains is below, with the dates the way she wrote them.*

---

## One season, verbatim

**Season-minus 40.** Nest in the blade root again. Same bird, I choose to believe, as last year. Protocol says evict. Protocol has never met this bird. Listed her in the manifest as DAMPER, AUXILIARY.

**Season-minus 30.** Tapped the tower at dawn. Two flat notes, two walked bolts, both replaced. The ridge shook out a third somewhere up the line — could hear it in the telegraph hiss, a wrong note in the ring. Passed it up the line. Keeper at 7 says pass it back next time with coffee.

**Season-minus 15.** Copper sky two dawns running. Saltmouth says the season's early. Saltmouth says this every year like it invented the observation.

**Season 3 (first wall).** Wall came up the southern foot at dusk: three kilometres of moving cliff, colour of a bruise with ideas. Gauge drum spun past its stop. Keyed Saltmouth: CLOSED UNTIL IT ISN'T. Watched it eat the flats from the nacelle with the lights off. Bird stayed under my jacket, weighing nothing at all, the way fear does.

**Season 11.** Hum went quiet mid-gust for eleven seconds. Eleven. I was up the mast with the crowbar before I decided to go. Hum came back like nothing. Bird screamed the whole time. Logged as: FELT PERSONAL.

**Season 19.** Vow to quit, annual, hereby sworn: this is the last season. Witness: bird. Bird declined to countersign, which is how I know she knows.

**Season 26.** Aurora behind the double dusk, violet on the old suns. The other keepers lit their lamps one by one down the ridge like we were answering something. Nobody keyed the network. We never do during. The Choir says the ridge hums in Third Bell when the sky does that. The Choir is not wrong, which I find worse.

**Season 31.** Wall out of nowhere, no dawn warning, sky rose and honest and then it wasn't. A courier came down the far crest ahead of it — running the wall, lights on, no time to do anything but which way I pointed. Pointed her at the scree line behind Turbine 6, the fast drop. She made the drop. Logged her plate as best the dust allowed: DRIFTLINE, partial, *07.*

**Season 40.** Closing the season. Bird fledged three. Bolts walked: forty-one. Walls: six. Vows sworn: one, expired.

If a courier named Rill ever reads this: the scree line behind 6 owes you nothing and you owe it nothing. That's the whole arrangement we have with this ridge. Come back slow sometime and drink water you didn't beg for. — *H.O.*
