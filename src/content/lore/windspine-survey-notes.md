*Row survey, kept by contract walker Bet Osuun, who walks all twelve towers of Windspine every ninth day with a slate, a spike wrench, and the row's only honest ladder. The walk takes eleven hours. The slate says what took longer.*

---

## Towers, in order walked

**T1–T2.** Nominal. Stamped at the bases: GUILD PROPERTY, REPAIRED UNDER DURESS. Someone has added, in paint that matches the stamp suspiciously well: SAME, FRIEND, SAME.

**T3.** Nest under the nacelle awning, occupied. Noted for the record: this bird has been listed in three consecutive surveys by three different walkers as NEST, ONE (1), which makes her, by row tradition, staff. Left alone. Staff is staff.

**T4.** Sixteen bolts gone from the service hatch, replaced — this is the part I keep re-reading — with *wooden pegs, whittled neat as choirboys.* The Reclaimers steal bolts. They do not whittle substitutes. Logged: THEFT, COURTEOUS. The Ridge keeps its own economy; we are allowed to notice.

**T5.** Drone in the brake housing, fifth-week running, pitch migrated up a third. The turbine is learning the scale the way the rest of us did: slowly, at work.

**T6.** Scree line holds. Logged gratitude to no one, per the arrangement. The arrangement is the ridge's and I do not punctuate it.

**T7.** The keeper's coffee debt to me stands at four tins and one apology, neither tendered. The tin situation is the ridge's longest-running weather system.

**T8.** Vibration readings wrong. Not high — *ahead*. The needle leads the gusts by a breath, like the tower is leaning into a wind that hasn't decided. Recalibrated the meter at base. At the crown it led again. Logged verbatim: THE TOWER KNOWS FIRST. Guild form has no box for that. Used the box for birds. It seemed right.

**T9.** Hala's vow to quit renews in forty days. Took odds with myself, paid myself, owe myself. The tower tapped two flat notes and a third I've started thinking of as *hers*.

**T10.** Pilgrim camped on the pylon, Choir, harmless, bolted to nothing, swears the tower hums his name — and here my slate holds a mark that is no script this row inks, though my hand set it down before my head agreed to it. Escorted down at dusk with tea. The pylon hummed the whole way down. We did not discuss it. There is tea, and there is the tower, and a survey is a list of the first kind.

**T11.** Bolt census: short by six. Peg census: up by six. Balanced, per the ridge's arithmetic. Stamped the page. The stamp felt like a courtesy someone was watching me perform.

**T12.** Nominal, except the anemometer spins on still air, afternoons only, like it's rehearsing. Logged: BEARING, probably. Under that: or remembering.

## Standing discrepancies

Readings across the row now lead storms by an average of one full breath and climbing. I have walked this row nine years. The wind used to surprise us. Now the towers file their objections early, and I am the only scheduled reader of the mail. If the Guild wants a forecast more accurate than the goat's, the form is called a survey and it is already being submitted.

*Filed, stamped, argued with, filed again. The goat's desk declined to comment.*
