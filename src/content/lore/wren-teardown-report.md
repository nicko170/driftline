*Pinned below the wall, season 68, in Ketch's hand, beginning profanely and ending quietly. Reproduced with permission, which he granted by saying "fine" without looking up.*

## Bench notes, the investigation

Kid asked me one too many times *why she pulls left* and I ran out of shrugs, so: full strip, laser plumb, level bench. The bench is level. I checked it with the level, the other level, and Nona Vex's level, which she wants back and is not getting back until it apologises. Pull at cruise: one point six degrees, port. Constant at load. Strongest in the hour after dawn. File that and keep reading, I did.

## The left knife theory

Port field-knife carries a regrind, two degrees shallow, somebody's repair from the middle seasons — the loud handwriting years. Theory: asymmetric knife, asymmetric field, pull. It is a beautiful theory. It explains everything cleanly and completely, which on this bench is disqualifying. I built a test vane to true spec, hung it, ran the field. Pull: unchanged. The theory died in trial and I held it the small funeral it deserved, one nod, no music.

## The Opinions, revisited

Left gyro cage, per the wall: Opinions, promoted long ago to Character. Rebalanced quarterly like the wall says. The wall stays. But for the record: with the cage dead-level on the rig, the ring settles a whisker off plumb *by itself*, settles there from any starting position, and hums a third higher while it does it. I checked the ring against the cradle-gauge twice and then I stopped checking, because a man can only be told the same thing by so many instruments before it becomes gossip.

## The dent

Port fin, under three resprays. Everybody asks. People who heard the radio play ask if it's from *Paloma's* run — pal, the ballad is a radio play — I was in it, disguised as myself, and I was the worst performance in it, and the show was free. But for the file: the dent is sand-struck, not glass-struck. Deep desert sand at wall speed, which means a storm, which means it went in her flank while she was *already* running flat out, which is a thing sand can only do from behind and above. I know of exactly one season the arithmetic fits. If the dent is hers, it is the only autograph on this machine I would never grind out, and I have stopped measuring it because I kept catching myself doing it gently.

## Findings

One: no fault found. Alignment true, knives hung, rings ringed. Two: the pull is editorial, as the wall has said all along. Three — and this goes in the report because this is a report and reports are where you put the thing you'd rather leave on the workbench — the pull eases on one heading. She runs dead straight toward the Cradle the way a compass needle runs toward north. Every Verdantine machine hums home. I have not told the kid. The kid compensates without thinking now, smiles while doing it, talks to her. Fine. That was always the fix.

*Verdict: she pulls left the way the rest of us lean — there's something over there worth leaning toward. Wall stays. Theory buried. Dent stays. — K.*
